01Overview

A Bail Office is a purchasable property in Grand Theft Auto Online added as part of the Bottom Dollar Bounties update.

02Description

The GTA Online Protagonist will initially receive a call from Maude Eccles. Having decided to retire, she seeks for someone to take over her bounty hunting business. The player can purchase one bail office location from a selection available on Maze Bank Foreclosures, with optional extras such as personal quarters, gun locker, additional agents, and cosmetic extras, as well as the ability to pre-purchase armor plating for the Bail Enforcement Burrito. Once purchased, it will serve the player as the in-game location of the Bottom Dollar Bail Enforcement business.

03Purchase

Upon entering the newly purchased business, the GTA Online Protagonist will be greeted by Maude making the finishing touches. She then introduces the player to her daughter, Jenette Eccles, who will now be acting as their assistant. As Maude takes her leave, Jenette energetically introduces the protagonist to their new business and the procedures associated with bounty hunting.

04Office

The office is the main area of the business. This is where Jenette, as well as two unnamed enforcement agents if hired, will be working. The holding cell is also located in this area, where bounty targets will be held for processing if brought in alive.

05Player's office

Behind Jenette's desk is the player's office. A computer there will allow the player to start bounty hunting missions. If purchased, the player's office will feature a small storage room used as the player's personal quarters. In the office, one of the desks will gain a plaque with a certificate of bail enforcement after the player has completed at least 10 bail target missions, a trophy of a golden sheriff star after completing at least 100 bail target missions, and a trophy of golden handcuffs after the player brings in all 24 unique bail jumpers alive. Also, completing four bounties will result in Jenette replacing the plant on the computer desk.

06Garage

Near the main entrance, there is a staircase leading into the garage, where the Bail Enforcement Van is stored. Two Personal Vehicles may also be stored inside the garage.
