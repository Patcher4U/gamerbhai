Little JacobLittle Jacob is a Jamaican arms/drug dealer who resides in an apartment in Schottler with his friend Real Badman. He and Roman Bellic are good friends, and Niko Bellic purchases weaponry from him when he gets a good friendship level with him. He serves as a helper and accomplice to Niko in some missions. He has a thick Jamaican accent, and besides the shootouts with his enemies he is a peaceful character.
Brucie KibbutzBrucie Kibbutz is a steroid-abusing fitness buff which is very proud of what he has achieved in wealth and body. In a "rage," he offers missions for Niko Bellic that involve racing vehicles and killing people. He is fairly wealthy and makes appearances on the radio. He sends Niko many emails and is the main character involved in both the racing and car theft side quests.
Patrick McRearyPatrick McReary is the youngest of the McReary siblings. He lives with his mother and sister in Meadows Park. He sometimes works for Elizabeta Torres, but has his own agenda after his brother's return to Liberty City. While working with Niko Bellic, he sets up a bank heist in Chinatown and the kidnapping of a Mafia boss' daughter in exchange of diamonds.
Dwayne ForgeDwayne Forge is an "old-school" gangster who's been in prison since the early 90s for crack distribution, only to be released into a completely unfamiliar environment, with an emphasis on "show-boating" and "bling". His life got completely out of hand after he went to prison, and claims he might want to kill himself for it. He needs Niko's help to rebuild his reputation.

Mikhail FaustinMikhail Faustin is the leader of his own Russian crime syndicate in Broker. Mikhail happens to be a heavy user of cocaine, and has a very short temper. He is also the best friend of Dimitri Rascalov.
Dimitri RascalovDimitri Rascalov is introduced as Mikhail Faustin's friend and associate. In later sequences, he is purported to be heading the rise of Russian immigrant organized crime in Liberty City.
Jimmy PegorinoJimmy Pegorino is the head of the Pegorino Crime Family in Alderney State. He is concerned about his family's decline and wants it to remain even with the Liberty City families. He is ruthless and intelligent, but watching his family dynasty crumble before him has made him erratic and dangerous.
Darko BrevicDarko Brevic was in Niko Bellic's old army unit during the Yugoslav Wars. He was one of the men that Niko came to Liberty City to find. Niko does some work for various people who intend to help him find Darko.
Bernie CraneBernie Crane is an old friend of Niko and Roman Bellic, though they knew him as Florian Cravic. He is one of the men Niko comes to Liberty City to find. Mallorie BardasMallorie Bardas is a Puerto Rican woman who works for Roman Bellic at his taxi firm. She and Roman are romantically involved. She is affiliated with people from South Bohan, including community leader Manny Escuela and drug dealer Elizabeta Torres.
Manny EscuelaManny Escuela is the South Bohan community leader. Throughout, he is shooting a documentary that shows how "he" is "cleaning up the streets" and spreading his message out to other communities, though he is actually taking credit for others' actions. Most real criminals consider him an annoying joke.
Elizabeta TorresElizabeta Torres is a powerful drug dealer from South Bohan. Her addictions eventually cause her to overreact and lash out at those around her. She has connections with other gangs involved with other criminals, including the McReary Crime Family through Patrick McReary and The Lost MC through Johnny Klebitz.
Francis McRearyFrancis McReary is a corrupt cop who is aware of felonies made by Niko Bellic. His missions normally involve a contract killing on a person who knows or has incriminating evidence against him. He is one of the McReary siblings, though the other brothers have a strong dislike of him.
Derrick McRearyDerrick McReary is the oldest of the McReary siblings, just returning from Ireland. He seems to have a background with the Irish Republican Army. He possesses a good knowledge of weapons and explosives, which is rivaled only by his knowledge of narcotics. He often laments about his tragic past and his regrets.
Gerald McRearyGerald McReary is the head of the McReary Crime Family and one of the McReary siblings, and the least idealistic. Gerry is a clever and dangerous mastermind who uses his information and leverage to prevent Francis from moving in on his other siblings.
Playboy XPlayboy X is an associate of Elizabeta Torres and is the head of cocaine distribution in North Holland. He is also friends with Dwayne Forge, who is serving a term in prison for drug trafficking. He wants to support the community and use the money he gains from drug sales to fund schools and community centers "once he gets rich," though it is obvious that he is fairly wealthy already.
Ray BoccinoRay Boccino is a tall, well-dressed, Italian man. He is ambitious and not very likable; Niko describes him as "slimy" even to the other criminals. Niko meets him through the McReary family. He is one of the three major players in the Pegorino Crime Family, an Italian crime family based in Alderney.
Phil BellPhil Bell is a half-Irish half-Italian front operator for the Pegorino family. He is one of the three major players in the Pegorino family, and the most likable of them. He is extremely paranoid, only speaking on the phone with code language. He operates his business through the Post OP Alderney Depot in Normandy.
United Liberty Paper contactThe United Liberty Paper contact is the alias of a shady government worker. He blackmails Niko into doing several dangerous jobs, trying to play the different criminal organizations off of each other. He says that he is not a "good guy," and that he only works for the "good guys."
Jon GravelliJon Gravelli is the dying head of the great Gambetti crime family, one of the five Italian crime families in Liberty City. He has gained enormous fame and power throughout his career, and is currently living out the remaining time of his life on a hospital bed under the care of U.L. Paper.
