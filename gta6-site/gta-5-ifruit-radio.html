01About iFruit Radio

iFruit Radio is hosted by Danny Brown and Skepta and plays afro-fusion, cloud rap, dancehall, electro, grime, jungle, rap, UK garage, wonky, and drum & bass. As of the Los Santos Drug Wars update, the radio only plays the Danny Brown playlist.
