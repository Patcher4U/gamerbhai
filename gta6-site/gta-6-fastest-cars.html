Fastest Cars in GTA 6: Truffade Thrax, Progen Emerus and the Top Picks

Everyone wants the fastest ride in Leonida. Guides agree on the top cars, but be careful: nobody has published proper tested top speeds yet.

01By the in-game Speed stat

Most guides put the Truffade Thrax first (about 8.7/10), then the Progen Emerus (8.3) and the Pegassi Vitello (8.2). One guide lists a modified Thrax at 9.2.

02By estimated top speed

Estimates name the Grotti Itali RSX and the Thrax as the top contenders, but the mph numbers differ between sites, so treat them as estimates.

03Get them free with cheats

Two of these are also cheat unlocks: the Truffade Thrax (finish all Shopping challenges) and the Progen Emerus (finish all Vehicle challenges). Codes for those aren't confirmed yet; see our cheat list for the 17 that are.

04Tip

Damaged cars sell for less, so if you're selling stolen cars to Moses, drive carefully.
