01The Original Heists

Players must be rank 12 or above to set up a heist, and must own a high-end apartment with a heist planning room. Once the player has reached the necessary rank and purchased an appropriate apartment, Lester Crest will contact them by text, followed by a phone call introducing the heists concept. The player must then meet with Lester at his garment factory. This will launch the tutorial heist, The Fleeca Job. For the purpose of the tutorial, Lester covers the up-front cost and acts as Heist Leader.

After completing the first heist, Lester will introduce a new character who will contact the player directly when a new heist is available. Following this, the player has the option of setting up heists themselves, becoming a Heist Leader. Each heist must be unlocked in order and requires an increasing amount of money to set up. A portion of this up-front cost goes towards paying crew members for taking part in the setup missions, although the Heist Leader does not receive any of this money and can profit only from successfully completing the heist finale.

The Heist Leader must have the requisite team to carry out the missions, typically consisting of 4 players (with the exception of The Fleeca Job, which requires a crew of just 2 players), and they will not be able to launch these missions with fewer than the number of players required. Likewise, the heist setup/finale will be failed if a crew member leaves mid-mission.

Heist lobby screen.

Some missions have all of the players involved working together as one unit, some require players to take on specific tasks like hacking or crowd control, while others require players to split into smaller teams to complete separate objectives concurrently. There are five heists that were added to GTA Online with the Heists update, each with its own series of setup missions. Four additional heists were added with subsequent updates, though these changed the formula of the original heists, including the structure of the setup jobs and adding the possibility to complete the heist with fewer players.

Unlike regular Contact Missions, payouts from heists (both setup missions and the heists themselves) are paid directly into the player's bank account, not their pocket as cash.

02The Doomsday Heist

The Doomsday Heist is a three-act heist added to GTA Online in the eponymous content update, released on December 12, 2017.

The heist is available to players who have registered as a VIP, CEO of an Organization, or President of a Motorcycle Club, as well as purchased an underground facility. After buying the facility, Lester will contact the player and ask them to meet him there, introducing them to Avon Hertz to initiate The Doomsday Heist.

The heist is split into three acts, each with its own setup missions and finale. Since The Doomsday Heist is more narrative-driven than the original heists, all acts must be completed in order on an initial playthrough, but afterwards they can be replayed in any order. This requirement only exists for the Heist Leader, as other players can join any setup mission or finale, regardless of whether they have completed them before. Furthermore, whereas the original heists required 4 players for both the setup missions and the finales, each act of The Doomsday Heist can be completed by a team of 2 - 4 players.

The Doomsday Heist introduces Prep Jobs, missions that take place in Freemode sessions and are required to unlock the setup missions. During Prep Jobs, players are tasked with stealing equipment that will be used during the heist (usually in the associated setup mission) and delivering it to their facility. However, since these missions occur in Freemode, other players can steal or destroy the equipment. If the player wishes to skip a Prep Job, they may choose to buy the equipment instead or steal it from other Organizations/MCs during their own Prep Jobs.

03The Diamond Casino Heist

The Diamond Casino Heist is a heist introduced in the eponymous content update, released on December 12, 2019.

Similarly to The Doomsday Heist, this heist is only available for registered VIPs, CEOs or MC Presidents. Following the introduction cutscene, where Georgina Cheng hires the GTA Online Protagonist and Lester to rob The Diamond Casino & Resort as payback against Thornton Duggan, players are instructed to purchase an arcade to be used as a front for planning the heist. Upon doing so, players can begin setting up The Diamond Casino Heist, selecting from three possible approaches.

Depending on the chosen approach, different setup missions need to be completed before unlocking the heist finale, although some of the setups are common across all approaches. Furthermore, unlike the original heists and The Doomsday Heist, there are a number of optional prep missions; these are not mandatory to unlock the finale, but generally help to facilitate it. Setup missions are structured similarly to the Prep Jobs for The Doomsday Heist, being short missions that take place in a Freemode session and involve stealing some piece of equipment to be used during the heist. Just like Prep Jobs, they can also be skipped if the player pays an up-front fee.

Because all setups for The Diamond Casino Heist are Freemode missions, they can be completed by a single player, with only the finale requiring a team of at least 2 players. This allows for a larger payout because the profits are split between less people, although a larger team can carry additional loot, increasing the potential overall take. Furthermore, players have to take into account the cuts of their heist support crew; these are NPC accomplices who function identically to the heist crew members in Grand Theft Auto V, providing support during the finale in exchange for a cut of the profits, which varies depending on their skill level.

04The Cayo Perico Heist

The Cayo Perico Heist is a heist added to GTA Online with the eponymous content update on December 15, 2020.

To initiate the heist, players must be a registered VIP, CEO or MC President, and they need to have met Miguel Madrazo at The Music Locker. After Miguel hires the GTA Online Protagonist to retrieve vital information on the Madrazo Cartel from notorious drug lord Juan "El Rubio" Strickler, players can begin preparations for The Cayo Perico Heist once they purchase a Kosatka submarine. The Kosatka serves as the operational center for the heist, and is where all the setup missions and the finale are launched from.

On the initial run of the heist, the player needs to gather intel on Cayo Perico by traveling to the island with English Dave and posing as a tour manager; on subsequent runs, they must steal a Velum 5-Seater and pose as one of El Rubio's smugglers. After sneaking around the island while avoiding the guards and finding various points of interest, including the primary target inside El Rubio's Compound, the player returns to San Andreas to complete the other setup missions. These are very similar to the setups for The Diamond Casino Heist, including the return of optional preps.

Unlike The Diamond Casino Heist, which had three possible approaches, The Cayo Perico Heist does not feature distinct approaches; instead, how the heist plays out is entirely dependent on the player's actions. Furthermore, crew members are no longer mandatory and do not take a cut of the final payout; instead, the player can choose to hire them for an up-front fee. Another change is the possibility to complete the entire heist solo, including all the setups and the finale. While this does not affect the reward for stealing the primary target, it reduces the amount of secondary loot (cash, artwork, weed, cocaine and gold) that can be stolen.

Primary Targets

During the setup mission Heist Prep: Gather Intel, players must obtain information about the contents of El Rubio's basement, which will determine their primary target during the heist. The potential targets are:

* - The target for the first playthrough, cannot be encountered on subsequent playthroughs. ** - The target is only available for a limited time.

Secondary Targets

In addition to the primary target, multiple secondary targets can spawn throughout Cayo Perico. These can be stolen during the heist to increase the final payout, though each player can only carry a limited amount of loot. The potential targets are:

  • Artwork
  • Weed

An additional $20,000 to $99,000 can be stolen from the safe in El Rubio's office inside the compound. Note that it does not take up space inside the lootbag.

05The Kortz Center Heist

The Kortz Center Heist is a heist introduced in the eponymous content update update for Grand Theft Auto Online, released on July 14, 2026.

The heist is available for registered VIPs, CEOs and MC Presidents who have purchased a Prix Luxury Mansion upgraded with an Art Studio. Its premise revolves around the Kortz Center in Pacific Bluffs, home to a sizeable art exhibition including numerous paintings, statues and other precious decorations. The GTA Online Protagonist is hired by Mr. Faber, on behalf of a European client, to raid the center and steal a rare and expensive painting kept in the vault with the aid of Raf De Angelis.

The heist is structured similarly to The Diamond Casino Heist and The Cayo Perico Heist. All setup jobs are Freemode missions that can be completed by a single player, with some of them being optional. The finale can also be completed solo, but bringing additional players allows for more secondary targets to be stolen. Among these, some are marked as "Buyer's Requests" and will sell for a higher price if taken.

The Kortz Center Heist makes some notable changes from previous heists, such as impacting the players' payout depending on how well they succeeded in erasing evidence of their crimes. Failure to delete the vault's CCTV footage or eliminate guards who witnessed the art theft will considerably reduce the primary target's value. Furthermore, the Heist Leader no longer decides each player's cut of the profits, which are automatically assigned by the game; however, they can choose whether to sell the primary target or keep it as a Mansion decoration.

Bonus Rewards

Along with the heist reward, the player can earn additional cash by completing the associated awards.

  • The Kortz Center Heist: Complete The Kortz Center Heist for the first time. $200,000 bonus.
  • Masterpiece: Complete the Elite Challenge for The Kortz Center Heist. $200,000 bonus.
  • Go Hard: Complete The Kortz Center Heist on Hard without losing a life. $200,000 bonus.
  • One Man Job: Complete The Kortz Center Heist with one player. $100,000 bonus.
  • Team Player: Complete The Kortz Center Heist with four players. $100,000 bonus.

Elite Challenges

The finale of The Kortz Center Heist comes with its own set of Elite Challenges that reward the player with an additional $50,000 if completed, or $100,000 if the heist is completed on Hard mode. Using quick restart during the finale will forfeit the elite bonuses.

The Elite Challenges are:

  • Complete in under 17:00 minutes
  • Value loss under $75,000
  • Shooting accuracy over 90%
  • Complete the Buyer's Request list
