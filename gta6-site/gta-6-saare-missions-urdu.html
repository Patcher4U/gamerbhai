GTA 6 Ke Saare Missions: Chapters aur Missable Side Missions (Urdu/Hindi)

GTA 6 ki story Prologue aur 5 chapters mein bati hui hai, takreeban 119 main missions. Yahan har chapter ka khulasa hai, bina story spoil kiye.

01Chapters ek nazar mein

Prologue (Leonida Penitentiary, Lucia): 1 mission. Chapter 1 (Leonida Keys, pehle Jason phir Lucia): 24 missions. Chapter 2 (Port Gellhorn, zyada tar Lucia): 18. Chapter 3 (Vice City, Jason aur Lucia): 32. Chapter 4 (Vice City, kai solo missions): 34. Chapter 5 (final chapter): 10.

02⚠️ Ye side missions miss ho sakte hain

Chapter 1: 'Get Shirley's Rum' aur 'Steal the Scooter' Keys chhodne se pehle kar lein, warna khatam. Chapter 3: Yago ki street race ('Race Against Yago') time-limited hai. Chapter 4: Shayna wala 'This Is a Free Call From...' (sirf Lucia) bhi time-limited hai.

03Zaroori side quests

Chapter 2 mein Billy ki 'Thrillbillies' questline (Mud Club se shuru), Bret ka 'Veteran Hunted' (sirf Jason) aur McKittrick Farm ki kahani. Chapter 4 mein 'That's Where The Money Is...' se bade bank vaults khulte hain.

04Har mission ki video

112 missions ke alag pages hain jin mein poori gameplay video hai, jaise Gone Fishing. Saare missions tarteeb se: walkthrough.

05Mission names ke baare mein

Missions ke naam aur tarteeb kai player guides se mila kar likhe gaye hain aur update ho sakte hain. Chapter 2 ke baad ke naam halka sa spoiler ho sakte hain.
