The Lab

📻 GTA 5 radio station · 13 songs

01About The Lab

The Lab is hosted by The Alchemist and Oh No, and plays hip-hop, rock, and electronic music.

The radio station was initially exclusive to the PC edition of Grand Theft Auto V, but later became available to PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, Xbox 360 and Xbox One versions of the game as part of the Ill-Gotten Gains Part 2 update on July 8, 2015.
