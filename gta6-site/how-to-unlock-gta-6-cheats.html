How to Unlock GTA 6 Cheats Fast: Every Unlock Goal Explained

Unlike older GTAs, GTA 6 cheats are locked until you complete a goal. Typing a code before that does nothing. Here's how to unlock the useful ones fastest.

01Finish the story first

Four cheats unlock when the main story ends: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather.

02Fitness and stunts

Body Armor: do 100 workouts. Unlimited Nitrous: complete 50 stunt jumps. Parachute: 50 skydives or BASE jumps from 300 ft or higher. No Fall Damage: survive a 40-ft fall 20 times.

03Shooting range medals

Get 25 medals at shooting ranges: bronze or better unlocks Max Ammo, silver or better unlocks Infinite Ammo, and gold unlocks No Reload.

04Collectibles

10 watches unlock Move Time Forward and 20 unlock Pause Time of Day. Finding all 24 Vice City Flamingos cards unlocks the Bubblegum filter.

05Remember

Cheats switch off trophies and saving, so save first. Get the full code list in our cheats guide and track every goal in the 100% tracker.
