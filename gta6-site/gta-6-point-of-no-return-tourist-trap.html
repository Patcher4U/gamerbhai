GTA 6 Point of No Return: Do This Before the Tourist Trap Mission

Chapter 1 ends with a mission that locks you out of the Leonida Keys for a long time, and players are losing side jobs, money and cars because nobody warned them. Here's what to do first. No story spoilers.

01Which mission is it?

Tourist Trap is the Chapter 1 point of no return. On the map it shows as 'Take Up Mattia's Offer' and it unlocks after the mission Recalibration. You still have free roam until you start it, so that's your last chance.

02Finish Shirley's side jobs

Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter disappear for good once Tourist Trap starts. Do them first.

03Protect your money (reported)

Guides report that Jason's checking balance is emptied when the chapter ends. Some say money moved into Family Savings is kept, so move or spend your cash before you go. This comes from early player guides, so treat it as reported.

04Your car and upgrades (reported)

Several guides say personal vehicles and their upgrades, even Ultimate Edition cars, are lost in the run of missions Tourist Trap starts. Don't spend big on upgrades right before it.

05Make a separate manual save

GTA 6 has no mission replay menu, so save in a separate slot before you select the mission. That save is your only way back to Chapter 1 free roam.
