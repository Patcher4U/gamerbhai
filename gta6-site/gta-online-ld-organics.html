GTA Online LD Organics Product

🌿 All 100 locations, one line each. LD Organics Product are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto Online, added with The Criminal Enterprises update.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are one hundred packages of product scattered throughout the state of San Andreas, located outdoors or inside various buildings and structures. They are white sealed bags with the LD Organics logo emblazoned on the front. Approaching a package does not make an audible sound unlike some other GTA Online collectibles, though approaching them will cause the player's controller to vibrate if playing with a controller, with the vibration growing more intense as they get closer to the package.

02Rewards

Each collected product rewards the player with $1,000 and 1,000RP , reaching a total of $100,000 and 100,000RP when all Product is collected.

To receive the final reward, the player must go to LD Organics in Chamberlain Hills to deliver the collected product. After entering LD Organics' side entrance, they exit the front door shortly after, smoking Weed and wearing the White LD Organics Tee and Black LD Organics Cap. The player is then awarded an additional $50,000, and the clothing worn is gifted to the player.
