How Many Endings Does GTA 6 Have? (Spoiler-Free)

Yes, GTA 6 has multiple endings. But you'll see very different numbers online. Here's why, and what shapes yours, without spoiling anything.

01What Rockstar said

Rockstar has described the story as having 'a lot more than two' possible endings. It hasn't given an exact number.

02Why the numbers differ

Player guides count anywhere from 6 to 9 endings; the most recent ones say 7 or 8. The gap comes from counting: some guides treat small variations of the same ending as separate endings, others don't. Claims of '18 endings' don't match any guide we could verify.

03What decides your ending

Guides agree it's not one menu choice at the very end: a big decision near the finale splits the story into routes, and your playthrough matters too. GTABase says your Criminal Profile plays a part.

04Protect your options

Make a separate manual save before the final chapter so you can go back and see other endings. GTA 6 has no mission replay menu. See Chapter 5 (spoiler warning) and the missables guide.

05No spoilers here

We won't describe the endings on this page. Mission names in later chapters may hint at events, so browse carefully.
