01Chapter overview

  • 📍 Area: Leonida Keys
  • 🎮 Playable: Jason, then Lucia
  • 🧾 Missions: 24

Jason earns his keep in the Keys doing odd jobs for Brian, from smuggling runs to rent collection. Lucia joins the story after her release.
