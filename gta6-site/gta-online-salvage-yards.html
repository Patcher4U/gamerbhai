GTA Online Salvage Yards

🏗️ Salvage Yards are purchasable properties in Grand Theft Auto Online added in The Chop Shop update.

01Overview

Salvage Yards are purchasable properties in Grand Theft Auto Online added in The Chop Shop update.

02Description

Salvage Yards are vehicle auto parts and scrap yard businesses under the name of Red's Auto Parts. They may be personalized with several additions, similar to most functional properties.

Players can choose from five salvage yards available from Maze Bank Foreclosures, after they receive a phone call from Yusuf Amir.

03Purchase

Upon entry, the GTA Online Protagonist takes a quick look around the salvage yard, and Yusuf Amir introduces himself and his taste for luxury cars. Yusuf's cousin and business manager Jamal Amir shows the protagonist the computer with Yusuf's wishlist of luxury car for his personal collection. The planning work for each car robbery is kept hidden, visible only in black light, on the wall behind the computer.

04Salvage Yard Robberies

Salvage Yard Robberies are a series of illegal jobs consisting of two preparations and the main job, which are executed in a similar manner to heists. These jobs consist of freemode preparations and the finale. Each robbery targets a specific car. The target vehicle and the type of the robbery changes every event week.

After the completion of a robbery, the player decides whether they ship the car to Yusuf via the Terminal, salvage the car parts, or keep the vehicle for themselves. The last option is only available if a specific vehicle is designated for this option during the event week.

05Tow Truck Service

The Tow Truck Service will allow players to earn both active and passive income by towing "abandoned" or derelict vehicles and salvage them for parts.

In addition to the money from sold parts, performing Tow Truck Service missions will increase the reputation of the Salvage Yard. Similar to the Popularity mechanic of the Nightclubs, a high reputation will significantly increase the daily income up to a maximum of $24,000 daily.
