GTA 6 Naye Players ke Liye 6 Zaroori Tips (Paise Na Ganwayein) - Urdu/Hindi

GTA 6 mein naye players aksar yehi galtiyan karte hain aur mehnat ka paisa kho dete hain. Ye 6 tips pehle parh lein.

011. Paisa bank mein rakhein

Jeb ka cash wasted ya busted hone par kho sakta hai. ATM aur BuckMe app aksar istemal karein, khaas taur par bare job se pehle.

022. Marne se pehle bechein

Chori ke jewels aur drugs fence par bech kar hi paisa bante hain. Pehle police se bachein; raste mein pakde gaye to poora bag gaya.

033. Apna look badlein

Ski mask, chashma ya topi heat kam karne mein madad karti hai. Getaway gaari tayyar rakhein aur search zone se jaldi niklein.

044. Gaari ko damage na karein

Damage hone se buyers kam paisa dete hain. Chori ki gaari mein tracker check karein.

055. Dono characters istemal karein

Zyada tar jobs Jason ya Lucia dono se hote hain, aur partner duffel bags uthane mein madad karta hai.

066. Stores ko restock hone dein

Ek hi store ko jaldi jaldi lootne se kam paisa milta hai. Alag alag jagah jayein. Aur tips: hidden controls (English) aur paise kamane ki guide.
