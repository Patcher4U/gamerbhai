GTA San Andreas Snapshots

📷 All 50 locations, one line each. Snapshots are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 50 Snapshots located all around San Fierro and city limits, which the player must find and photograph using a Camera. The Camera has several spawn points in San Fierro, all located near Snapshot locations.

The Snapshot location indicators appear as a rotating circular camera icon, only visible when viewed in first-person through the Camera's viewfinder. Between 00:00 and 05:00, a pinkish glow can be seen without the Camera, which marks where the snapshot icons are. Outside of these hours the glow can be seen using Night Vision Goggles.

Each Snapshot taken rewards the player with $100, totaling $5,000 for photographing all Snapshots. Photographing all 50 Snapshots is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Snapshots are available upon gaining control of Carl Johnson during In the Beginning (collecting them before San Fierro is unlocked is risky due to a 4-star wanted level obtained). The player is informed of their availability at the end of the mission Photo Opportunity.

03Rewards

Collecting all 50 Snapshots will reward the player with additional $100,000 bonus (totaling $105,000), and spawn points for the Shotgun, Micro SMG, Sniper Rifle and Grenades in front of the Doherty Garage.

The Doherty Garage items, after collecting all Snapshots.
