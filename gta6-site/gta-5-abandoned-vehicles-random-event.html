01Abandoned Vehicles – location 1

WhereSmoke Tree Road, Grand Senora Desert
Who can get itAll characters
What happensA lone Surfer sits idling on a desolate dirt road near the airfield. As the player approaches the van, a man will appear behind the player and stun him. The screen will turn black, and the player will wake up wearing only underwear over a railway line with a train approaching him quickly. Alternatively, if the player as Trevor quickly activates his special ability, the player can shoot the man, to which he will defensively claim he was "Just playing."
RewardNo reward. Alternative: A Stun Gun.

02Abandoned Vehicles – location 2

WhereRON Alternates Wind Farm
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player will find a Journey parked on a dirt road in the wind farm, with a couple of male rednecks having sexual intercourse inside. If disturbed, they'll turn hostile, leave the vehicle and try to shoot the player.
RewardNo reward.
