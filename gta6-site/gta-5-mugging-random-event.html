GTA 5: Mugging

🌴 3 locations · A random thief will steal the wallet of a pedestrian. Chase the thief and return the wallet to the victim, or keep it.

01Mugging – location 1

WhereChamberlain Hills, Strawberry Avenue
Who can get itAll characters
What happensA random thief will steal the wallet of a pedestrian. Chase the thief and return the wallet to the victim, or keep it.
RewardKeep cash: $200. Return cash: $20 and a full special meter. If the player kills the victim afterwards, they will get another $91.

WhereMeteor Street, Hawick
Who can get itAll characters
What happensA random criminal is attacking a woman in a alley. Simply kill him before he kills the woman.
RewardIf saved, the victim says she can get the player Corkers tickets but she simply flees and no reward is received.
