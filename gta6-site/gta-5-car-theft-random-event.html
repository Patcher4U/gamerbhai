01Car Theft – location 1

WhereGreat Ocean Highway, North Chumash, near the Hookies bar
Who can get itAll characters
What happensA woman will have her car stolen from her. The player will need to chase down the criminal and return the car to the woman.
RewardA unique pink Peyote or +5 Driving skill.

02Car Theft – location 2

WhereExceptionalists Way, Los Santos International Airport
Who can get itAll characters
What happensA woman will have her car stolen from her. The player will need to chase down the criminal and return the car to the woman.
RewardA unique dark red modified Rocoto or +5 Driving skill, and an email from the woman saying that she's delivered a luxury suit to the player's wardrobe.*

03Tips & notes

  • Regardless of the option taken (between taking the vehicle or returning it) the outcome is the same. The woman will respond differently in the email she sends the player, depending on the choice. Therefore, the player can take the vehicle and earn a new suit but will not earn +5 driving skill.
