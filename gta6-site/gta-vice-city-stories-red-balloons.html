GTA Vice City Stories Red Balloons

🎈 All 99 locations, one line each. Red Balloons are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 99 Red Balloons located all around Vice City, which the player must find and burst using firearms, melee weapons or explosions. The player has to use manual aim for most of the balloons and some can be placed so high up that the player is forced to use a Sniper Rifle to get a decent shot.

This is a reference to the 1984 hit song by Nena called "99 Luftballons", released in English as "99 Red Balloons", which was featured on the radio station, Wave 103 in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

Bursting all Red Balloons is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Red Balloons are available upon gaining control of Victor Vance during Soldier.

03Rewards

Each Red Balloon burst will award the player with $250. Bursting all balloons alone will result in a total of $24,750 earned.

For each 10 Red Balloons, a permanent spawn point for a specific item is created in front of 101 Bayshore Avenue, The Compound and The Clymenus Suite safehouses. Additionally, before Conduct Unbecoming is passed, the first six rewards will spawn at the barracks.
