GTA 5: Altruist Cult Shootout

🌴 1 location · After delivering four victims to the Altruist Cult, the Altruists will take Trevor hostage upon receiving the fourth…

01Altruist Cult Shootout

WhereChiliad Mountain State Wilderness, Altruist Camp
Who can get itTrevor only
What happensAfter delivering four victims to the Altruist Cult, the Altruists will take Trevor hostage upon receiving the fourth victim and try to use him as a sacrifice. Trevor will then need to kill all the Altruists and make his way out of the camp.
Reward$25,000 in four different briefcases around the camp (total of $100,000).

02Tips & notes

  • Note 1: This event is unable to be completed if the player runs out of victims to deliver.
  • Note 2: Aside from the money, the camp also has an RPG that respawns every few minutes. Sticking around after killing the cultists is a good way to obtain an RPG early in the game, and build up a cache of otherwise-expensive ammo.
  • Note 3: This random event does not count for the 57/60.
  • Random Events that are exclusive for returning players to the Enhanced Edition.
