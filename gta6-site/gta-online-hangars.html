GTA Online Hangars

✈️ Hangars are purchasable properties in Grand Theft Auto Online added in the Smuggler's Run update.

01Overview

Hangars are purchasable properties in Grand Theft Auto Online added in the Smuggler's Run update.

02Description

Hangars are large aircraft storage facilities. They may be customized with several additions, similarly to Bunkers, Biker Clubhouses and CEO Offices.

There are five hangars available to purchase from Maze Bank Foreclosures.

Once a hangar is purchased, an introduction cutscene is played upon first entry. The protagonist discovers two corpses near an unoccupied Cuban 800. Ron Jakowski, using an intercom speaker in the hangar, warns the player that he has a space weapon (Russian). The protagonist eventually finds him behind a stack of boxes, speaking through a microphone and watching through a small CCTV monitor. Ron is startled by the protagonist and gets scared until realizing they are his new business partner. He goes on to describe how he is alone and was supposed to be protected forever by his best friend who has "gone Vinewood" on him. Ron then reveals he is the one who killed the two (assumed) gorvernment agents and thinks he screwed up. Eventually, Charlie Reed, the mechanic, appears from a room of the hangar with a Burger Shot bag and a thermos flask in his hands, and he is briefly introduced by Ron before being sent back to work. From there, Ron gives the player a full tour of the hangar, explaining how the facility works, showing the office, the living quarters (if purchased), the supply storage and the mechanic, who is able to modify owned aircraft. After the tour, both Ron and the player load the corpses into Ron's plane while he states he will be in touch with the player once they start the business after taking care of some radio work.

03Trinkets

With the release of the San Andreas Mercenaries update, the room next to Charlie Reed's workshop will fill up with various trinkets from completing LSA Operations and the Project Overthrow missions. On top of the desk, there are several trinkets unlocked by completing the bonus objectives of each LSA Operation, while the bottom desk and the floor have certain equipment used and cargo crates collected during Project Overthrow. These trinkets are as follows:

  • LSA Operation - Direct Action: Merryweather key card and tracking device (can also be from LSA Operation - Surgical Strike).
  • LSA Operation - Whistleblower: Nightvision goggles, Charlie Reed's case files, a hard drive and USB.
  • Reporting for Duty: Black cargo crate.
  • Falling In: Orange cargo crate.
  • Breaking Ranks: Small Coil crate.
  • Unconventional Warfare: Black parachute bag.
  • Shock & Awe: Big Coil crate with Avi Schwartzman's key card on top of it.

04Functionality

The office provides the player with access to Free Trade Shipping Co to run their smuggling business. However, the player can simply use the hangar and workshop to store and modify aircraft without undertaking any smuggling operations.

If the player has purchased a hangar at Fort Zancudo, then they receive low-level access pass clearance into the base, whereby they will be allowed to drive or fly into the base without incurring a wanted level. Additionally, all members of Organizations or Motorcycle Clubs will receive the clearance if the CEO or President owns a Zancudo hangar, though it will be revoked if the player leaves or is removed from the MC or Organization.

A complimentary Cuban 800 is supplied with every hangar.

05Storable Vehicles

Below is a list of every available aircraft that the player can store in their hangar. At the rear of the hangar is a Personal Aircraft Management menu, which can be used to move and display the aircraft on the floor of the hangar and move them to and from an unseen storage location.

Aircraft that can be stored in a Hangar are classified as small, medium, large or extra large. A vehicle's classification dictates how many overall vehicles can be stored on the floor of the hangar. There is on-floor capacity for twelve small, five medium, two large and one extra large aircraft, or various combinations of sizes (except for the extra large category). Attempting to bring in a new aircraft with no spare parking on the floor for that size will move it to storage immediately. Hangar capacity including the unseen storage facility is 20 total aircraft, regardless of their size. On 4 March 2025, 15 extra slots were made available if the player owns the McKenzie Field Hangar. GTA+ members got 5 extra Vinewood Club Storage slots that do not require purchasing the McKenzie Field Hangar.

The player cannot store stolen aircraft, CEO/VIP helicopters or aircraft provided in missions. In addition, the player cannot store the Thruster or Sparrow, as they belong to dedicated properties.
