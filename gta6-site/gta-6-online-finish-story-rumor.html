Do You Need to Finish GTA 6's Story to Play GTA 6 Online? (Fact Check)

A post claiming Rockstar will lock GTA 6 Online until you finish 100% of the story (every stranger mission and collectible) went viral this week. It isn't true. Here's what's real.

01The rumor

The post said Rockstar 'confirmed' that players need 100% single-player completion, including all side missions and collectibles, before they can join GTA 6 Online servers. It spread fast on X and in YouTube comments.

02Where it came from

Mashable traced the claim to a satirical account. Rockstar Games and Take-Two have announced no such requirement, and no official source mentions one.

03Is GTA 6 Online out?

No. GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 as a single-player game, with no online mode at launch. Rockstar has not given a release date or window for GTA 6 Online yet.

04What about 2027?

You'll see '2027' in a lot of videos. That timing comes from industry talk and reporting, not from a Rockstar announcement, so treat it as a guess until Rockstar confirms.

05What you can do now

Enjoy the story at your own pace. Use our mission walkthrough, the cheat codes and the 100% tracker. We'll update our GTA 6 Online release date page the moment Rockstar announces anything.
