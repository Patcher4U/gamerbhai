GTA 6 Mein Paise Kaise Kamaye? Har Chapter ka Best Tareeqa (Urdu/Hindi)

GTA 6 mein paisa story ke saath saath khulta hai. Har chapter mein kya karna chahiye, yahan asaan alfaaz mein. Raqam players ke andaze hain aur patch ke baad badal sakti hai.

01Chapter 1: shuruaat

Illegal operations lootein (gang hideouts) aur maal Brian ya Donnie ko bechein. Key Lento mein Exotic Animals front ke back room ki safe ka code nahi chahiye. Kenton ke treasure dives: koi goli nahi chalata, har wreck takreeban $1,000+.

02Chapter 2: gaariyan aur hunting

Port Gellhorn mein Moses ko chori ki gaariyan bechein, takreeban $1,000 fi gaari (tracker na ho, damage na ho). Bret Vogel ke liye hunting: sirf Good ya Pristine janwar, $500 se kuch hazaar tak. Yasmani ke jewelry jobs: $10,000 se $25,000.

03Chapter 3-4: bara paisa

Randy ki high-end car thefts: $30,000+ fi gaari. Chhote banks ('Credit Check' mission ke baad): $5,000 se $45,000. Pilot Pete ke air drops (sirf Jason): $10,000 se $20,000. Aakhir mein 8 bade bank vaults: har ek $205,000 tak.

04Hamesha kaam aane wala

Demolition derby baar baar khel sakte hain: 5-6 minute, jeetne par takreeban $3,800 se $6,300.

05Paisa bachaayein

Cash ATM ya BuckMe app mein jama karein, warna wasted/busted hone par kho sakta hai. Poori table aur image: money guide. Naye players ke liye: 6 zaroori tips.
