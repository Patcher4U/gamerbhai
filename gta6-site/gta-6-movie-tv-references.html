GTA 6 Movie & TV References: Scarface, Dexter, Fast & Furious and More

Rockstar has always filled GTA with nods to classic films, and Leonida is no exception. These are the movie and TV references players have found so far, with where to look. No story spoilers.

01Scarface: the motel room

The most famous one. An Ocean Beach motel room, Apartment 3C, recreates Scarface's infamous hotel scene, right down to the chainsaw in the bathtub. (Some guides also claim a Scarface-style mansion on Starfish Island, but that isn't confirmed yet.)

02Dexter: Trinity Apartments

Near the top of Washington Island in Vice City, Trinity Apartments nods to Dexter's Trinity Killer, and the building looks a lot like Dexter's own apartment.

03Fast & Furious: Brian's Eclipse

A green 1995 Mitsubishi-style Eclipse sits in a garage on Vespucci Island, a nod to the car Brian O'Conner drove in the first film.

04Miami Vice, Heat and Goodfellas

A Rockstar developer named Miami Vice, Heat and Goodfellas as touchstones for the game, and you can feel it: players spotted an outfit that makes Jason look a lot like Miami Vice's Sonny Crockett.

05Smaller nods players reported

A 'trunk shot' from inside a car (a classic Tarantino/Pulp Fiction camera angle), a Collateral-style taxi ride, a Top Gun joke in the map name Goose Key, an X-Files trophy and a Seinfeld 'ASSMAN' licence plate. Some of these come from a single guide, so treat them as reported.
