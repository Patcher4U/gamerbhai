GTA 6 Update 1.002.002: Patch Notes, Crash Fixes and What's Still Broken

Four days after launch, Rockstar pushed GTA 6's first update. It's small, but it fixes some of the most annoying launch bugs.

01Version and size

01.002.002 on PS5 (about 604 MB) and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S (about 1.6 GB), released 23 November 2026.

02What's fixed

Several crashes (Rockstar didn't say which), story phone calls that didn't trigger and could block progress, autosave problems, weather getting stuck for long periods, and a Wanted Level you couldn't lose. Some outlets also list a gym deadlift fix and a partner loot-bag fix.

03Not fixed yet

Rockstar's notes don't mention the Xbox bug that makes the game run at double speed, and there's no performance change (still 30 FPS). Rockstar also hasn't said whether progress lost before the patch can be recovered.

04Crashing on PS5?

If you see error CE-108255-1, install the update, keep your console's system software updated, and rebuild the database from Safe Mode if it continues. Always save manually before big missions.
