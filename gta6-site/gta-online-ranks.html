GTA Online Ranks & RP

⭐ RP for ranks 1–120 and what each one unlocks.

01How ranking works

You earn RP (Reputation Points) for almost everything in GTA Online: missions, races, heists, stunts, even driving against traffic. Each rank needs more RP than the last. Ranking up unlocks new weapons, clothes, tattoos, vehicle mods and jobs.
