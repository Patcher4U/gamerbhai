01Drunk Drivers – location 1

WhereAguja Street, La Puerta
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player will find a man trying to take his drunk friend home. Just give the drunk guy a lift to his home in Alta Place, Hawick.
Reward$80. After being dropped off, he seems to drop dead and can be looted. Player can also keep the Baller.

02Drunk Drivers – location 2

WhereArmadillo Avenue, Sandy Shores
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player will find a drunk couple trying to head back to their motel. Just give them a lift to the motel.
Reward$40. Player can keep the Emperor.

03Tips & notes

  • Note 1: If playing as Trevor, the player can take both the man and the couple to the Altruist Cult (but not at the same time as they're two separate events) instead and receive $1,000 for each of them. (The drunk couple also counts as if two people were brought to the cult, which means $2,000 will be paid out).
  • Note 2: If playing as Franklin he'll break the fourth wall and tell the player "See, kids, this why you shouldn't get wasted."
