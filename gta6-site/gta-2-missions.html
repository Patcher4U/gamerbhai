★How GTA 2 missions work

GTA 2 (1999) is split into three areas: Downtown, Residential and Industrial. In each area three gangs give jobs from coloured payphones: green phones are the easiest, yellow are harder and red are the hardest. Working for a gang raises your respect with it and lowers it with its rivals – and a gang that hates you shoots on sight. Earn the points target for the area to unlock the next one. Names with a slash are the mission’s two titles (in-game name / alternative name).
