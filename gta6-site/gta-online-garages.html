GTA Online Garages

🚗 Every GTA Online garage – low-end, medium and high-end – with address, price and notes.

01Overview

Garages are purchasable Safehouses in Grand Theft Auto Online.

02Description

Garages can be purchased by the player via the Dynasty 8 website, or by interacting with property sale signs outside their respective buildings. Previously, garages could only be purchased after the player has reached Rank 5. However, as of the Bottom Dollar Bounties update, this requirement has been removed. If configured to show in the Interaction Menu "Hide" options, a Garage available for purchase will show on the map with .

Unlike Apartments, these properties are for exclusive purpose of vehicle storage, with limited player activities available inside. Garage locations of all tiers (low-end, medium, and high-end) are largely equally spread between Los Santos and Blaine County.

Garages can store Personal Vehicles either purchased from in game websites or dealerships, or low end vehicles and bicycles stolen directly off the street. Purchased vehicles are automatically fitted with a tracker and insurance, but stolen vehicles will need these manually purchased from Los Santos Customs. Driving an un-tracked vehicle into an owned garage will automatically fit the tracker for free.

  • After the High Life Update, a 2nd property could be purchased.
  • After Festive Surprise, a 3rd could be purchased.
  • After the Lowriders update, enhanced version players can own a 4th.
  • After the Executives and Other Criminals update, enhanced version players can own a 5th.
  • After the Bikers update, enhanced version players can own a 6th.
  • After the Los Santos Summer Special update, enhanced version players can own up to 8.
  • After The Criminal Enterprises update, enhanced version players can own up to 10.

03Garage Tiers

The Dynasty 8 listings are divided into three tiers of purchasable properties; low-end, medium and high-end.

04Low-end

Low-end garages have a basic, dingy interior with poor lighting and various tools and machine parts scattered around. A laptop and radio are located on the rear workbench for the player to interact with. These garages can store a maximum of two Personal Vehicles and one Bicycle.

05Medium

06High-end

High-end garages feature premium quality lighting and an all-white interior, with a more pristine work area. Most of the Garage Mechanic's vehicle maintenance and mechanical equipment are inaccessible and separated behind a glass wall at the rear of the garage. Like the other tier garages, the rear workbench features a laptop and radio, but a wall-mounted TV has also been added to the left of the workbench, allowing the player to watch TV and CCTV, and spectate other players. These garages can store a maximum of 10 Personal Vehicles and three Bicycles, which are stored on dedicated wall racks. Note that though there are six total bike racks, only three may be stored.

07Eclipse Boulevard Garage

Added with the Los Santos Drug Wars update, the Eclipse Boulevard Garage is listed as a high-end garage, but features significant differences to other high-end garages. The property can hold up to a total of 50 Personal Vehicles, features customizable interiors similar to Office Garages, access to a roof patio, and a kitchenette area on each floor that offer Snacks and other amenities.

08Interactions

Garage interiors feature very limited interactions compared to Apartments, but do contain the ability to:

  • Browse the Internet
  • Use the radio
  • Watch TV (High End garages only - including option to spectate other players in the lobby)
  • Manipulate the stored vehicles into different storage spots
  • Eat and collect Snacks (Eclipse Boulevard Garage only)
