GTA 6 Mein ATM Kaise Churayein? Best Gaari Contender (Urdu/Hindi)

GTA 6 ka zabardast paisa trick: gaari mein winch lagayein, ATM se jorein aur ukhaad kar le jayein. Best gaari aur poora tareeqa yahan hai.

01Best truck: Contender

Forbes ke mutabiq Contender abhi tak sab se kaam ki gaari hai. Ye Kingfishers gang ke adday par milti hai. Isay rakhne ke liye Randy ki car list quest khatam honi chahiye.

02Winch zaroori hai

Mod shop se winch khareedein (Contender ya zyada tar trucks par lagta hai). Phir ATM ke paas reverse karein, hook lagayein aur kheench lein.

03ATM kahan hain

Map par Robberies > Yasmani's ATM Jobs mein. Winch wali gaari chalate hue hare ATM icons nazar aate hain. Ek guide ke mutabiq 25 ATMs ukhaade ja sakte hain.

04Paisa kaise banayein

ATM se Marked Bills milte hain jo Yasmani se milne ke baad hi bik sakte hain. Aur tareeqe: paise kaise kamaye. English: ATM guide.
