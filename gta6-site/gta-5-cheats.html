01How to use cheats

  1. PlayStation / Xbox: press the buttons quickly during gameplay (not in the pause menu). A message confirms the cheat.
  2. PC: press ~ (tilde) to open the cheat box, type the code and press Enter.
  3. Phone: press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, open the keypad and dial the number.
⚠️ Cheats turn off trophies/achievements for that play session only. They don't work in GTA Online, and some are blocked during missions.
