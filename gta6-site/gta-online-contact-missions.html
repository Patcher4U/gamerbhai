01How contact missions work

Missions are unlocked as the player ranks up in GTA Online — to start with, few will be available, but this number will increase with rank. Higher-ranked missions tend to be more difficult, take longer and offer greater money ($) and reputation point (RP) rewards. Missions are offered to the player in the form of a text message sent by one of the NPC boss characters. These messages are sent periodically to the player, and may offer either new missions or previously completed assignments (but tend to weigh towards the former should any new, uncompleted jobs be available). Players also have the option to phone boss characters themselves and request a mission (by selecting "Request Job" on their phone), shortly after which they will receive a text message offering one of that character's missions. In any case, the player can only personally start missions for which they've reached the appropriate rank, although they can be invited to missions that they cannot access themselves by higher ranking players who have unlocked those missions.

After the tutorial is finished, online missions can generally be played in any order the player wishes, so this list does not reflect any required chronological order of completion. However, certain mission strands added with later content updates tend to form standalone narratives and therefore must be played in a specific order; upon completing the strand for the first time, the missions can usually be replayed in any order from the pause menu. Such strands include Lamar's missions added with the Lowriders update, the Casino Story Missions, A Superyacht Life, the Short Trips, Operation Paper Trail, First Dose/Last Dose, Project Overthrow, The Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid, Oscar Guzman Flies Again, Mr. Faber Work and KnoWay Out.
