01How to use cheats

  1. Press Up on the D-pad (or the phone key on PC) to take out Niko's phone.
  2. Open the keypad, dial the number and press call.
  3. After the first use, the cheat is saved in the phone's Cheats menu so you can turn it on again quickly.
⚠️ Using cheats blocks some achievements/trophies (for example "One Man Army" and "Cleaned the Mean Streets"), even after you save.
