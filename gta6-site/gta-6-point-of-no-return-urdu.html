GTA 6 Point of No Return: Tourist Trap Se Pehle Ye Zaroor Karein (Urdu/Hindi)

Chapter 1 ke aakhir mein ek mission aapko Leonida Keys se lambe arse ke liye door kar deta hai. Bohat se players side jobs, paisa aur gaariyan kho rahe hain. Pehle ye kaam kar lein. Koi spoiler nahi.

01Kaunsa mission?

Tourist Trap. Map par ye 'Take Up Mattia's Offer' naam se dikhta hai aur Recalibration mission ke baad khulta hai. Shuru karne se pehle tak free roam hai, wahi aakhri mauqa hai.

02Shirley ke jobs khatam karein

Get Shirley's Rum aur Steal the Scooter Tourist Trap shuru hote hi hamesha ke liye khatam ho jaate hain.

03Paisa bachaayein (reported)

Guides ke mutabiq chapter khatam hone par Jason ka checking balance khaali ho jata hai. Kuch kehte hain Family Savings mein rakha paisa bach jata hai. Ye early player guides ki maloomat hai.

04Gaari aur upgrades (reported)

Kai guides kehti hain ke personal gaariyan aur unke upgrades (Ultimate Edition wali bhi) is mission ke baad chali jaati hain. Is se pehle mehenge upgrades na karein.

05Alag manual save banayein

GTA 6 mein mission replay menu nahi hai. Mission shuru karne se pehle alag slot mein save karein, wahi Chapter 1 mein wapas jaane ka rasta hai. English: point of no return.
