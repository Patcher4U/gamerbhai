03Beginner tips (read before you lose your cash)

🏦 Bank your money

Cash in your pocket can be lost when you're wasted or busted. Use ATMs and the BuckMe app often, and especially before a big job.

🎒 Sell before you die

Stolen jewels and drugs only become money at a fence. Lose the cops first. If you get busted on the way, you lose the whole bag.

🧢 Change your look

A ski mask, glasses or a hat helps lower your heat. Have a getaway car ready and get out of the search zone fast.

🚘 Don't wreck the car

Damage lowers what buyers pay. Check that stolen cars have no tracker and match what the buyer asked for.

👫 Use both of them

Most jobs work as Jason or Lucia, and your partner helps grab duffel bags. Some side work is for one character only.

⏳ Let stores restock

Hitting the same store again too soon pays less. Rotate your targets.

04GTA 6 on PC: what we actually know

No PC version has been announced. GTA 6 is out only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since 19 November 2026. Take-Two's investor schedule lists only those two consoles up to 31 March 2027.

On 13 October 2026, GamesBeat reported (citing unnamed sources) that PC could come in early 2027. Rockstar didn't comment. In the past, Rockstar's PC versions arrived 7 to 19 months after console. See the full history on our PC page.
