GTA San Andreas Unique Stunt Jumps

🏍️ All 70 locations, one line each. Stunt Jumps are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 70 jumps located all around the San Andreas state. Each completed stunt jump earns the player a reward of $500, making this a way to earn some cash early in the game, with up to $17,500 on offer from stunt jumps in the initially available regions, namely Los Santos and Red County. In total, the player can earn $35,000 by completing every stunt jump all over the state.

Additional Insane Stunt Bonus payments can often be earned as the player completes each stunt jump.

Even though they appear in the statistics, stunt jumps do not count towards 100% completion.
