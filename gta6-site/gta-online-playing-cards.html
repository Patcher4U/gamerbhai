GTA Online Playing Cards

🃏 All 54 locations, one line each. Playing Cards are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto Online, added with The Diamond Casino & Resort update.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are fifty-four cards scattered throughout the state of San Andreas, located outdoors or inside various buildings, such as 24/7 stores.

02Rewards

Once collected, they give the player chips to use in The Diamond Casino & Resort, as well as RP. The first five collected cards each give 100 chips, with each next one giving 50 chips more than the previous; the final card gives the player 2550 chips. The total amount the player receives for collecting all cards is 66,650 chips.

Collecting all cards will also grant the player a special High Roller outfit, a new deck of playing cards on the private table inside the Master Penthouse, a new decoration for the Master Penthouse, and an exclusive reward in Red Dead Online that will get them a Cardenas Poncho for free. (The promotion to get a free Collector's Bag was a limited-time event and ended October 15th, 2019.)

Collecting all cards also gives players access to the "High Roller" exit disguise to use for The Big Con approach in The Diamond Casino Heist.
