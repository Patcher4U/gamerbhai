02Rewards

The reward for 100% completion in GTA IV is a "100% Game Completion." message in the top left corner and the ability to buy an unlimited amount of ammunition for all weapons, although it still costs the same as before, and the Key To The City achievement is unlocked. This reward has a bug that is present when the player loads a 100% saved game, which causes all the ammo that was over the original limit to be lost.
