GTA San Andreas Oysters

🦪 All 50 locations, one line each. Oysters are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 50 Oysters located all around San Andreas, which the player must find and collect while swimming.

Each Oyster collected rewards the player with $100, totaling $5,000 for collecting all Oysters alone. Collecting all 50 Oysters is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Oysters are available upon gaining control of Carl Johnson during In the Beginning (oysters in San Fierro/Whetstone/Flint County area before The Green Sabre is completed as well as Las Venturas/Bone County/Tierra Robada before completing Yay Ka-Boom-Boom are extremely difficult to collect due to a 4-star wanted level (5-stars at Easter Basin Naval Station and Area 69 even after unlocking San Fierro and Bone County respectively) obtained).

03Rewards

Collecting all 50 Oysters will reward the player with:

  • Additional $100,000 reward, totaling $105,000.
  • Maximum Lung Capacity stats.
  • Ability to date girlfriends regardless of the current Sex Appeal, Fat or Muscle stats.
  • Ability to reencounter and date girlfriends after a cancelled relationship (except for mission-related girlfriends Denise Robinson and Millie Perkins).
