02What happens

OG Loc is released from prison and CJ, Sweet, and Big Smoke go to the police station to pick him up. Loc reveals that a Mexican gangster, Freddy, raped him in jail and stole his rhymes. Loc goes to confront Freddy at his house. Pick up OG Loc from jail, chase and kill Freddy, and take OG Loc to Burger Shot.
