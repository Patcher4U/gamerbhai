GTA 6 Easter Eggs Found So Far: UFO, Skunk Ape, Jet-Ski Dog and More

Players have barely started exploring Leonida and the secrets are already piling up. Here are the most talked-about finds. None are officially confirmed by Rockstar, so treat locations as player reports.

01🛸 UFO abduction

Reported near Port Gellhorn after 2 a.m. in-game. A classic GTA mystery is back.

02🦍 The Skunk Ape

GTA 6's version of Bigfoot, reported in the swamps of Grassrivers.

03🐕 The jet-ski dog

A dog on a jet ski leads you to a hidden island full of dogs and toys, where it digs up a wallet with $1,218.

04🥚 A real egg nest

A nest of eggs sits on top of the Vice-Dale County radio tower: an Easter egg that is literally eggs.

05📺 Pop-culture nods

An apartment on Washington Island looks a lot like the Bay Harbor building from Dexter, and Bug Stars, the pest-control company from GTA 5, is back.

06🎮 Old GTA references

GTA 5's yoga teacher Fabien LaRouche shows up in a magazine, and there's a recreation of the classic Vice City cover pose.
