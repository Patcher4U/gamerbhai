GTA 3 Rampages

💀 All 20 locations, one line each. Rampages are challenges started via collectibles in Grand Theft Auto III.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are a total of 20 Rampages located all around Liberty City, appearing as a rotating white skull pickup item, which the player must find and collect while on foot and not in an active mission to trigger the challenge.

The Rampages are a spiritual successor of the Kill Frenzy challenges from the previous titles.

Each Rampage has two spawn locations; if the player fails a Rampage at its first spawn, it will spawn at its second set location, and if the player fails it again, it will spawn back at the first location.

Completing all 20 Rampages is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Objectives

After starting the Rampage, the player's inventory will be locked to a specific weapon (with infinite ammo) provided for the challenge. The player is given the objective of reaching the target kill count within 2 minutes.

The kill count and time limit countdown are shown on the HUD while the Rampage is active. After the Rampage is completed/failed, the player's previous inventory items are restored.

The following objectives are given by the Rampages:

  • Kill a specific number of gang members using the provided weapon in 2 minutes.
  • Perform a specific number of headshots using the provided weapon in 2 minutes.
  • Kill a specific number of gang members in a drive-by shooting with a Uzi in 2 minutes.
  • Run over a specific number of gang members in a player-chosen vehicle in 2 minutes.
  • Destroy a specific number of vehicles using the provided weapon in 2 minutes.

03Availability

The Rampages are available upon completing Luigi's Girls.

04Rewards

Each completed Rampage rewards an increasing $5,000. For example, the first Rampage offers $5,000; the second offers $10,000, etc., up to $100,000 and a bonus of $1,000,000 for completing all 20.
