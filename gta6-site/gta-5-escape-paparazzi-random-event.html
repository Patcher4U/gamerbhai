01Escape Paparazzi

WhereVinewood Boulevard, Downtown Vinewood
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player will find a celebrity surrounded by paparazzi and asking for help. Find a vehicle and lose the paparazzi, then just take her home. If the player resorts to violence (run over the paparazzi or threat them with a gun), Lacey will call him crazy and run away. If the player steals the Vader or the Cavalcade near the paparazzi, they will mistake him for a celebrity and surround him to take photos, depending on the character: Michael for Mitch Dexter, Franklin for MC Clip, Tony McTony, Morgan Chester and Bill Anthony and Trevor for Jock Cranley. Lacey will use this chance to run away. If the player refuses to help her, she will take the Rumpo or Vader parked nearby and drive away. If she crashes or stops for too long, she will abandon the vehicle and hijack a new one. In all cases, the mission will be abandoned.
Reward$750.

02Tips & notes

  • If playing as Trevor, he can take the woman to the Altruist Cult instead and receive $1,000.
