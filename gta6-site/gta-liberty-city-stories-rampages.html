GTA Liberty City Stories Rampages

💀 All 20 locations, one line each. Rampages are challenges started via collectibles in Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are a total of 20 Rampages located all around Liberty City, appearing as a rotating white skull pickup item, which the player must find and collect while on foot and not in an active mission to trigger the challenge.

If the player fails a Rampage, they are allowed to immediately restart it at its original spawn point. Upon completing a Rampage, the pickup will disappear.

Completing all Rampages is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Objectives

After starting the Rampage, the player's inventory will be locked to a specific weapon (with infinite ammo) provided for the challenge. The player is given the objective of reaching the target kill count within 2 minutes.

The kill count and time limit countdown are shown on the HUD while the Rampage is active. After the Rampage is completed/failed, the player's previous inventory items are restored.

The following objectives are given by the Rampages:

  • Kill a specific number of targets using the provided weapon in 2 minutes.
  • Run over a specific number of targets in a player-chosen vehicle in 2 minutes.
  • Destroy a specific number of vehicles using the provided weapon in 2 minutes.

03Availability

The Rampages are available upon completing Home Sweet Home.

04Rewards

Each completed Rampage rewards $50, multiplied by the number of Rampages completed. For example, the first Rampage offers $50; the second offers $100, the third offers $150, etc. A spawn point for the M60 is unlocked in front of all safehouses upon completing all Rampages.

M60 at the Portland Safehouse, after completing all Rampages.
