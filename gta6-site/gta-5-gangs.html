GTA 5 Gangs

🔫 Every gang in GTA 5, where they hang out and how they fight.

01Overview

This page lists all the gangs, private militia and other criminal organizations around Southern San Andreas in Grand Theft Auto V.

There are eleven major gangs (including street gangs and drug cartels), four minor gangs, and a criminal organization led by Trevor Philips.

02Ballas

The Ballas are a large African-American street gang. They mainly operate in Davis and have three sets:

  • Original Covenant Ballas (OCB) – A Balla set that controls the western part of Davis, with their hotspots for activity being Grove Street and Covenant Avenue.
  • East Side Ballas (ESB) – A Balla set that controls the eastern part of Davis, with they hotspots for activity being Brouge Avenue and Davis Avenue.
  • South Rancho Ballas (SRB) – A Balla set based in an unknown part of Los Santos, with their clothes suggesting that their territory is in the southern part of Rancho.

03Los Santos Vagos

The Los Santos Vagos, also known as LSV or the Vagos, are a large Mexican street gang said to be the largest Hispanic street gang in Los Santos. They are rumored to have connections with some major Mafia groups, according to The Underbelly Of Paradise TV program. The Vagos are supporters of the LS Pounders, as they wear clothes with the team's logo and a yellow colour to match.

The Vagos control almost the entire neighborhood of Rancho. Jamestown Street and the Rancho Projects in the southern part of Rancho are also particular hotspots for Vagos activity, as well as various backalleys like Cypress Flats. They have three sets:

  • North Side Vagos (NSV) – Controls the north side of Rancho. They are probably at war with the Varrios Los Aztecas, due to Northern Rancho also being an Azteca territory. They can be sometimes seen in the northern part of Jamestown Street and also in the territories of the Original LS set.
  • Original LS Vagos (OLSV) – Controls the south side of Rancho. They are probably at war with the Ballas, mainly the South Rancho Ballas set, due to the proximity of the territories. They can be seen in the southern part of Jamestown Street.
  • East Side Vagos (ESV) – Controls the Cypress Flats and also the east side of Rancho. They can be seen in central Cypress Flats and also in the territories of the other sets.

04Varrios Los Aztecas

The Varrios Los Aztecas, also known as VLA or simply the Aztecas, are a relatively small Mexican street gang in Los Santos, which can be identified by their light teal/turquoise coloured cholo-style clothing. The VLA are in competition with Trevor Philips Enterprises over the weapons trade in Blaine County. After Trevor destroys Ortega's trailer, the VLA decide to seek revenge. Several Aztecas attack the Sandy Shores meth lab, but end up killed by Trevor and Chef.

Varrios Los Aztecas members can be found in their territory east of the Central Los Santos Medical Center around northern Rancho, and in Blaine County, with their headquarters being the Alamo Sea area.

05Marabunta Grande

The Marabunta Grande is a Salvadoran street gang involved in drug trafficking. They are rivals of the Varrios Los Aztecas, competing against them to monopolize the drug trade and arms trafficking in Blaine County. They can be identified by their full-face tattoos and bandanas on their heads, faces, necks and wrists. Marabunta Grande members can be commonly found in their territories in El Burro Heights and Vespucci Beach.

The Marabunta Grande play a minor role in the storyline, appearing only in the mission Father/Son, where they steal Michael's boat after his son Jimmy tried selling it to them. They also feature as potential enemies in the arms trafficking side missions.

06The Families

The Families are a large African-American street gang based around the areas of South Los Santos. They have territory in the Chamberlain Hills and Strawberry neighborhoods, and also some parts of Davis. They hold a bitter rivalry with the neighboring Ballas and Los Santos Vagos. Their signature green attire comes mainly from their support of the colours of the Los Santos Corkers Baseball Team and the Feud Baseball Team. Their sets are:

  • Forum Drive Families (FDF) – More commonly referred to as the "Forum Gangsters", they are the secondary set of the Chamberlain Gangster Families, founded by Franklin Clinton and Lamar Davis shortly before the events of the game.
  • Carson Avenue Families (CAF) – Hostile to the Chamberlain Gangsters and presumably other sets. Their territory consists of the Strawberry section of Carson Avenue, though they sometimes appear in Chamberlain Hills. Members of this set often sport varsity jacket attire with the letter "C" for Chamberlain, which also stands for "Corkers", the baseball team supported by the gang.
  • Davis Neighborhood Families (DNF) – Their territory consists of the northwest part of Davis. They currently are at war with the East Side Ballas set, due to proximity of the territories. They are known as "Dirty Davis Devils" or "Davis Devils". They can often be seen around Chamberlain Set territories (mainly Forum Drive) and sometimes south and northwest of Davis.

07Madrazo Cartel

The Madrazo Cartel, also known as the Madrazo Crime Family, is a Mexican-American drug cartel headed by Martin Madrazo. The gang muscle is mainly made up of young Hispanic men of Guatemalan origin. Gang muscle are seen wearing distinctive Flying Bravo polo shirts of varying colors and designs, usually with numbers on the sleeves.

The cartel members at Martin Madrazo's residence, La Fuente Blanca, will not be immediately hostile to the player unless the player stays in their vicinity too long. Verbal warnings will be given before any aggressive response is encountered.

The cartel plays a minor role in the storyline, first appearing in the mission Marriage Counseling after Michael unknowingly destroys a house belonging to Martin, who forces him to pay for the damages. They are later seen in Caida Libre, when Michael and Trevor visit Martin's mansion for a job, and briefly at the end of Monkey Business, when Trevor reluctantly returns Martin's wife Patricia Madrazo after kidnapping her.

08Armenian Mob

The Armenian Mob is a criminal group operating around scrapyards and construction sites. Not much is known about them. They can easily be identified by their brown leather jackets, dress suits, gold chains and thick accents.

Simeon Yetarian presumably has connections with them, as they provide him protection, as seen in his random event.

09Kkangpae

The Kkangpae, also known as the Korean Mob, is a South Korean criminal organization based in Los Santos. They are a small yet very dangerous group of Korean mobsters who are involved in a wide range of criminal activities. They can usually be found on Ginger Street in Little Seoul.

The Kkangpae do not play any important role in the story. However, they sometimes attack The Hen House business in Paleto Bay, whether it belongs to Michael, Franklin, or Trevor.

10Los Santos Triads

The Los Santos Triads, also known as the Wei Cheng Triad, are a large Chinese crime syndicate led by Wei Cheng, originating from Yangshan in China. Their most notorious front is the Raven Slaughterhouse in Cypress Flats.

The Triads are secondary antagonists in the game, developing a rivalry with Trevor, whom they initially sought out for a potential business partnership to expand their operations into Blaine County. However, they ultimately decide to partner with the O'Neil Brothers instead due to Trevor's unstable and unpredictable nature, which results in Trevor killing most of the O'Neils and destroying their ranch.

As revenge for taking out his business partners and assaulting his son, Wei Cheng sends his men after Trevor. However, they instead capture Michael, whom the Triads plan to use to lure Trevor into a trap. When this fails due to Trevor's refusal to save Michael, the Triads try to dispose of him, but Michael is rescued by Franklin. At the end of the game, Franklin kills Wei Cheng and his bodyguards at the Pacific Bluffs Country Club, ending the Triads' attacks against Trevor.

11The Lost MC

The Lost Motorcycle Club is an outlaw motorcycle club operating on both the East and West Coasts of the United States, as well as the Midwest. They own a club hideout in Stab City, known as The Range. The Lost also own a safehouse/front in Grapeseed.

The Lost's primary businesses in Blaine County became the illegal distribution of firearms and drugs. This causes tension between them and Trevor Philips Enterprises, which escalates into a full-blown war after Trevor murders Johnny Klebitz and numerous other members of The Lost. Trevor eventually seizes many of the club's assets for himself, including the Sandy Shores Airfield, and destroys The Range to prevent The Lost from retaliating.

Although The Lost maintain a strong presence in Blaine County, they also happen to be active in the city of Los Santos. They are mostly seen around the East Vinewood area, where they patrol in their Gang Burritos and operate a clubhouse near the Vinewood Casino.

12Merryweather Security

Merryweather Security Consulting is a private military company and security contractor which appears as a prominent enemy group in several missions. The company was founded by Don Percival and is dedicated to various military operations. According to their website, Merryweather operates all around the world but has a particularly strong presence in San Andreas.

Merryweather suffers many problematic setbacks at the hands of the game's protagonists, particularly Trevor Philips. By the end of the story, Merryweather's reputation is in shambles after so many catastrophic failures, causing several of their regular clients to drop them from their payroll, including the U.S. government.

13Hippies

The Hippies are a small gang inspired by the American subculture of the same name. They have a fascination with extraterrestrial life as they are seen at alien-related landmarks.

The Hippies are found in a trailer park north of RON Alternates Wind Farm, and in Beam Me Up, an art installation located on the hill east of Sandy Shores. They are easily distinguished by their sleeveless shirts, or lack of a shirt, bandanas, tattoos on their chest and back, and necklaces. Hippies are normally not aggressive towards the player, as they are unarmed and will typically run away at the first signs of violence.

14Rednecks

The Rednecks are a loose affiliation of rednecks found throughout much of Blaine County. Rather than being one organized gang, they appear to be a collection of rural citizens, with some being apart of criminal factions such as the O'Neil Brothers, Larry Tupper's cronies, and several border patrol groups, among others.

Its members are exclusively white males, and consist of either thin young men or overweight aging men. Most rednecks have tattoos and show signs of methamphetamine use.

15O'Neil Brothers

The O'Neil Brothers are a family of inbred, hillbilly, redneck meth dealers from Grapeseed, Blaine County, led by Elwood O'Neil and his brothers. They are familiar with the Varrios Los Aztecas and The Lost MC, as they are all involved in the sale of drugs and illegal firearms throughout Blaine County. They also share a bitter rivalry with Trevor Philips Enterprises.

After some potential clients, the Los Santos Triads, decide to work with the brothers instead of Trevor due to his violent and reckless behavior, Trevor is angered so much that he attacks the O'Neil Ranch and single-handedly kills most of the family, save Elwood, Walton and Wynn, who left before the attack. He also destroys their ranch by blowing up the meth lab in the basement. Following this, Trevor starts receiving phone calls from one of the brothers, threatening him with death. Eventually, Trevor, with the help of Michael, Franklin, and Chop, tracks down and eliminates Elwood, Walton and Wynn.

16Altruist Cult

The Altruist Cult is a malevolent religious cult comprised entirely of elderly men from the baby boomer generation that follow Altruism and believe that the generations after them are the cause of all problems in the world. Their main headquarters is the Altruist Camp, in the Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness. It is a small but functional fortified village with numerous buildings painted with the Altruists' livery.

They are first mentioned after the mission Nervous Ron, when Ron Jakowski claims that a cult is requesting "lost souls" in exchange for cash. It is implied that the cult are cannibals, as Ron says that they "just want them for dinner" and are obsessed with "young blood". Trevor has the option to deliver new victims to the Altruists in exchange for cash, until the cult eventually double-crosses and tries to murder him, only to be wiped out.

17Street Punks

The Street Punks are a minor African-American street gang, whose members are commonly found throughout Los Santos and Blaine County.

18Civil Border Patrol

Joe and Josef, the two sole members of the Civil Border Patrol.

The Civil Border Patrol is a civilian militia that operates in Blaine County and aims to "protect" America from "dangerous" illegal immigrants. The group consists exclusively of two members: Joe, a racist and paranoid southern man who despises immigrants; and his friend Josef, a cold, violent Russian immigrant who barely speaks any English.

19Trevor Philips Enterprises

Trevor Philips Enterprises is a business run by Trevor Philips. It is a Blaine County-based company that primarily sells drugs and illegal weapons to other crime syndicates.

While the group deals mainly with drugs, Trevor wants to expand the gun trade by shipping firearms to the drug cartels in Mexico. Even though Trevor calls it an enterprise or industry, the "company" is run mainly by four people: Trevor, serving as its CEO and main muscle; Wade Hebert, who provides information and some muscle; Chef, who cooks meth and is used as muscle occasionally; and Ron Jakowski, who works as Trevor's operations chief, informant and pilot (although at a rookie level). All of them started working for Trevor shortly before the events of the game.
