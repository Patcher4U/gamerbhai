01How GTA Advance works

GTA Advance (2004, Game Boy Advance) is a top-down GTA set in Liberty City a year before GTA III. You play as Mike, who is out for revenge after his partner Vinnie is apparently killed. The story moves from Portland to Staunton Island and Shoreside Vale, with jobs from Vinnie, 8-Ball, Jonnie, King Courtney, Cisco, Asuka Kasen and others.
