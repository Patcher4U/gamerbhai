02What happens

Michael, Franklin, and their accomplices infiltrate the FIB Headquarters to retrieve incriminating information against Steve Haines. Michael seizes the opportunity to delete his own criminal record, destroying Haines' leverage over him. The heist can be performed in two ways: Covert Approach or Roof Approach, based on the player's choice in Architect's Plans.
