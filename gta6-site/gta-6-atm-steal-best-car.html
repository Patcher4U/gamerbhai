GTA 6 Best Car to Steal ATMs: The Contender and How ATM Jobs Work

One of GTA 6's best money tricks: hook a winch to an ATM and drive off with it. Here's the best truck for the job and how the whole thing works.

01The best truck: Contender

Forbes calls the Contender the best all-round vehicle so far. It spawns at the bases of the Kingfishers (the first enemy gang you meet). To keep it, you need to have finished Randy's car list quest.

02You need a winch

Buy a winch at a mod shop for the Contender or most other trucks. Then back up to an ATM, hook it on and pull it out of the wall.

03Finding ATMs

ATM jobs show on the map under Robberies > Yasmani's ATM Jobs, and green ATM icons appear while you drive a winch-equipped vehicle. One guide counts 25 towable ATMs.

04Cashing out

ATMs give Marked Bills. You can only fence them after you've met Yasmani, so do his early jobs first. More cash ideas: money guide and Urdu money guide.

05Lose the heat

Expect police attention. Have a getaway route, change your look and bank your money afterwards. Tips: hidden controls (duck in the car with Circle / B).
