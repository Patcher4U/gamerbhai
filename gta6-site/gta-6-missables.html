GTA 6 Missables: Every Side Mission You Can Miss (By Chapter)

GTA 6 has a few side missions that vanish for good if the story moves on. Here's every confirmed missable, chapter by chapter, so you don't lose any. No story spoilers.

01Chapter 1: before Tourist Trap

Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter disappear once you leave the Leonida Keys. Tourist Trap ('Take Up Mattia's Offer') is the point of no return. Full checklist: point of no return guide.

02Chapter 2: optional questlines

Not reported as missable, but easy to forget: Billy's Thrillbillies (starts at the Mud Club), Bret's Veteran Hunted (Jason only) and the McKittrick Farm side story. See the Chapter 2 walkthrough.

03Chapter 3: Yago's street race

Race Against Yago is time-limited, and Yago's race chain ends with 'Brother to Brother'. Do it as soon as it appears so you don't risk the rest of the chain. Chapter 3 walkthrough.

04Chapter 4: Shayna's call

This Is a Free Call From... is a Lucia-only, time-limited pickup. Also don't skip That's Where The Money Is..., which unlocks the big bank vaults (up to $205,000 each). Chapter 4 walkthrough.

05How to never miss anything

Keep a separate manual save before every chapter ends (there's no mission replay menu), answer phone-call pickups when they ring, and track progress with our free 100% tracker. Urdu: saare missions.

06A note on accuracy

This list combines several player guides and is updated as more players finish the game.
