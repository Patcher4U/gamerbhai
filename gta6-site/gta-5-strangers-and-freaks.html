GTA 5 Strangers and Freaks

🌴 66 side missions from 18 characters · ✅ = counts for 100%

01How Strangers and Freaks work

Strangers and Freaks are side missions given by odd characters you meet around Los Santos and Blaine County. They show up on the map as a ? icon in the colour of the character who can start them (blue for Michael, green for Franklin, orange for Trevor). There are 66 listed here; 24 of them count towards 100% completion.
