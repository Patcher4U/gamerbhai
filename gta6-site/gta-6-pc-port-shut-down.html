Unofficial GTA 6 PC Port Shut Down: 3,000+ Repos Taken Down by Take-Two

If you saw videos of GTA 6 'running on PC' this week, that project is gone. Here's what happened and why you shouldn't download any 'GTA 6 PC' files.

01What happened

On 24 November 2026 Take-Two filed a DMCA takedown against Leonida, the biggest unofficial project trying to run the Xbox version of GTA 6 on Windows. GitHub removed 3,007 repositories (the original and its forks) and the project's Discord server was taken down too.

02Why

Take-Two argued the project told users to 'bring your own files', which it says pointed to leaked game files. The developers say they didn't share the files themselves.

03Is GTA 6 playable on PC?

No. There's still no playable PC version and no official PC release date. GTA 6 is out only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Some other fan recompilation projects reportedly still exist but run poorly.

04⚠️ Don't download 'GTA 6 PC'

Any 'GTA 6 PC download', 'crack' or 'port' you find now is either a scam, malware, or leaked files that can get you into legal trouble. Never enter passwords or card details on those sites.
