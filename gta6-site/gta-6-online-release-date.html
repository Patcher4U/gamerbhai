GTA 6 Online Release Date: Is Multiplayer Coming? (What's Confirmed)

GTA 6 launched as a single-player game. So when can you play online with friends? Short answer: nobody officially knows yet.

01What Rockstar has said

Nothing about a date. Rockstar hasn't announced a GTA 6 Online mode at all, and its 19 November launch post covered the story, soundtrack and launch bonuses without mentioning multiplayer.

02Where '2027' comes from

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg he expects GTA multiplayer next year. That's his expectation, not a Rockstar announcement.

03What about GTA Online (GTA 5)?

Take-Two says it will keep supporting the existing GTA Online after GTA 6's release, so it isn't shutting down.

04Beware scams

Any site offering "GTA 6 Online early access" or a "beta invite" is a scam. Read our scam alerts.
