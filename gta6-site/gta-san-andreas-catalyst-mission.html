02What happens

After failing to find the stuff he buried, Ryder prepares his drugs. C.R.A.S.H. arrives and informs that an ammo train will be arriving shortly at East Los Santos. However, when CJ and Ryder get to the location, the Vagos and the Ballas can be seen attempting to rob the train as well. Steal crates of weapons from a train.
