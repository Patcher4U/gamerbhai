GTA Vice City Hidden Packages

📦 All 100 locations, one line each. Hidden Packages are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 100 Hidden Packages located all around Vice City, which the player must find and collect while on foot. These items cannot be collected while on a vehicle.

Their design seems to be based on mid-century modern Mesoamerican resin figurines. After completing the mission Rub Out, a broken hidden package will appear on the bar counter at the Ocean View Hotel room, revealing the package contains a white powder.

These statues are a reference towards the twelfth episode of the first season of Miami Vice, "Milk Run", where similar cocaine-filled statues are used for smuggling.

Collecting all Hidden Packages is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Hidden Packages are available upon gaining control of Tommy Vercetti during In The Beginning...

03Rewards

Each Hidden Package will award the player with $100. Collecting all packages alone will result in a total of $10,000 earned. An additional $100,000 reward is obtained upon collecting the last package, totaling a $110,000 reward.

For each 10 Hidden Packages collected, a permanent spawn point for a specific item is created at the Ocean View Hotel, Vercetti Estate and Hyman Condo safehouses.

Spawn points for military vehicles and aircraft are also unlocked, including the Sea Sparrow, Rhino and the Hunter.

  • $100,000
  • Hunter spawns at the Fort Baxter Air Base (alternatively unlocked after Keep Your Friends Close...).
  • Hunter spawns at the Ocean Beach Helipad (requires Keep Your Friends Close... to be completed beforehand).
