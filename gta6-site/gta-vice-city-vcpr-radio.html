01About VCPR

Vice City Public Radio (VCPR) is a public talk station. It has only one program, Pressing Issues, which is hosted by Maurice Chavez. The two station supervisors, Jonathan Freeloader and Michelle Montanius, appeal listeners for funds during breaks. Each segment focuses on a particular issue, with Chavez chairing a discussion on the issue between several guests with different backgrounds, points of view or approaches.

Three such issues are broadcast within the game. They are:

  1. Morality (guests: Pastor Richards, Jan Brown, Barry Stark)
  2. Perception and Positive Thinking (guests: Konstantinos Smith, Jeremy Robard, Jenny Louise Crab)
  3. Public Safety (guests: Alex Shrub, Callum Crayshaw, John F. Hickory)
