01Protagonist

CharacterWho they are

After the murder of his father, Huang is called to Liberty City by his Uncle Wu "Kenny" Lee to deliver a family 'heirloom", the Yu Jian Sword, to secure his family's claim to the leadership of the Triad gangs. 02Main Characters

CharacterWho they are
Wu "Kenny" LeeYu Jian – Salt in the Wound The leader of the Lee Clan within the Triad organizations of Liberty City, Uncle Kenny is from the old school. He likes his suits double breasted, his platitudes from a fortune cookie, and his payments in cash. Uncle Kenny wants control of the Triads, and there is little that he won't ask Huang to do for him to get it.
Wade HestonRaw Deal – Salt in the Wound A dirty narcotics detective with the IAD on his tail and a monkey on his back, Wade Heston forms a dubious alliance with Huang Lee to find the mysterious FIB rat who has set up both Huang and Wade. Lying, stealing and murdering are all in a day’s work for this member of Liberty City’s finest.
Hsin JaomingTrail Blazer – Salt in the Wound Hsin Jaoming has ruled the Triad gangs of Liberty City for over twenty years with an iron fist. But now nearing retirement, he is looking for a worthy successor with as much ambition and brains as himself.
Chan JaomingPimp His Ride – Clear the Pier The son of Liberty City Triad boss Hsin Jaoming, Chan is a willful, drug-addled and stupid playboy. Chan buys his friends and makes his enemies with little caution and even less thought.
Zhou MingStealing the Show – Hit from the Tong Zhou Ming is the leader of the Ming Family and might be another rival of Huang Lee and the Lee Family.
Lester LerocDouble Trouble – See No Evil Lester Leroc is a private investigator who is working for Hsin Jaoming. He is undercover with the Angels of Death to find out if there is a rat in the Angels. He asks Huang to help him out with some things he can't do himself.
Rudy D'AvanzoGrave Situation – A Rude Awakening Rudy D'Avanzo is an Italian gangster. He contacts Huang, saying he knows who the rat is. He tells that it's name is Jimmy Capra. Rudy asks Huang to do some things for him in exchange for a tape with evidence Jimmy is the rat.
Melanie MallardParking Pickle – Sources Melanie is a character exclusive to the PSP version of Chinatown Wars. She is a documentary film maker who had a deal with Chan Jaoming. She is killed by Chan for not obscuring their faces when she reveal them.
Ling ShanPursuit Farce – Under the Gun Ling Shan is a street smart Chinatown girl whose beauty belies her deadly skills. She serves as Uncle Kenny's master at arms, and provides necessary muscle in a town that only respects money and brute force. Trained in the martial arts and deadly with all manner of small arms, it is Ling who teaches Huang the combat skills he will need to survive on the streets of Liberty City…
Xin ShanDeadly Xin – Xin of the Father Xin Shan is a Chinese gangster. After you found the two Lions of Fo, you are able to download his missions at the Rockstar Social Club. He asks you to help him out with some things, because his partner was killed. He is the brother of Ling Shan.

04Random Characters

CharacterWho they are
AlonsoRandom Encounter Alonso is an insane man who employs Huang in driving him to the bus station, where he attacks a Bus, believing it to be a dragon.
CherieRandom Encounter Cherie is a prostitute who attempts to kill Huang, believing that he wanted to sleep with and murder her.
Giorgio2 Random Encounters Giorgio is a hot dog vendor who gets Huang to plant a tracking bug on his wife's car, suspecting her of cheating. Upon tracking her down, he discovers that she has become a prostitute.
GuyRandom Encounter Guy is an aspiring porn star who asks Huang to drive his Limo to the Porn Idol contest at Perestroika while he and eight women "prepare" in the back.
Marcy2 Random Encounters When Huang first meets Marcy, she has been told that she doesn't have long to live, so asks to be taken on a reckless joyride. When they next meet, she says that she had been misdiagnosed, and asks to be taken to her lawyer's office. She is ironically run down upon arrival.
Selma2 Random Encounters Selma is an eccentric woman who hires Huang to take her to her date. When they next meet, she starts stalking Huang.
Tommy2 Random Encounters Tommy is a paranoid xenophobe who asks Huang to hijack an Ammu-Nation truck so that he can get weapons "to protect himself from terrorists". When they next meet, he goes on a rampage, getting both him and Huang wanted by the LCPD.
Wilhelm3 Random Encounters Wilhelm is a businessman who twice pays Huang to drive him to City Hall to purchase property. When they next meet, Wilhelm has become bankrupt, and commits suicide by jumping from the Dukes Bay Bridge.
