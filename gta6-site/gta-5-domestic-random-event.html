01Domestic

WhereDidion Drive, Vinewood Hills
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player will find a man being kicked out his house by his wife. Wait at the kerb for the man to retrieve his golf club, then give him a lift to where he wants to go.
Reward$80 and the man becomes a new golf opponent with a Hard skill level.

02Tips & notes

  • If playing as Trevor, he can take the man to the Altruist Cult instead and receive $1,000. However, this is not recommended as the player will permanently lose out on having Castro Lagano as a golf opponent.
