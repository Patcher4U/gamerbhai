GTA 5 · 100% COMPLETION

GTA 5 100% Checklist

🌴 In order to achieve 100% Completion in Grand Theft Auto V, the player must complete 69 storyline missions, 20 strangers and freaks missions (Franklin only), 14 random events, 42 hobbies and pastimes and 16 miscellaneous tasks.

✅ Tick each task as you finish it – your ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Achieving 100% Completion

  • Complete all 24 of Franklin's Strangers and Freaks missions. The following is a list of Strangers and Freaks and their respective missions that count towards 100% completion, each adding a 0.5% completion progress unless explicitly stated otherwise
  • Barry
  • Beverly Felton
  • Dom Beasley
  • Peter Dreyfuss
  • Hao
  • Mary-Ann Quinn
  • Omega
  • Tonya Wiggins
  • Complete 42 out of the 61 Hobbies and Pastimes. The following is a list of hobbies and pastimes that count towards 100% completion
  • Complete all 18 Shooting Range challenges
  • Complete 16 miscellaneous tasks. The following is a list of tasks that count towards 100% completion
  • Friend activities

02Rewards

Note that not all of these rewards instantly appear. It may be necessary to play for some time before the UFOs appear and the trigger point for The Last One becomes available. This is the second Grand Theft Auto game since Grand Theft Auto Advance in which no weapon or vehicle-related award is given for 100% completion.
