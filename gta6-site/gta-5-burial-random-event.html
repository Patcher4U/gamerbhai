01Burial

WhereNorth Point, Paleto Bay, Blaine County
Who can get itAll characters
What happensTwo guys are trying to bury a woman alive. The player will need to kill the two men and then give the woman a lift to her destination.
RewardUp to $600 from each killed enemy, and a full special meter. The victim's family will also give the player $60,000 sometime later.

02Tips & notes

  • If playing as Trevor, he can take the woman to the Altruist Cult instead, but will only receive $1,000.
