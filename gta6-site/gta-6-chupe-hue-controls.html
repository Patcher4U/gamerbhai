GTA 6 Ke Chupe Hue Controls aur Tricks (PS5 aur Xbox) - Urdu/Hindi

GTA 6 ka control scheme bohat bara hai aur tutorial bohat kuch nahi batata. Ye sab se kaam ke chupe hue controls hain (default layout).

01Ulti gaari seedhi karein

Gaari ke side par jaa kar Square / X daba kar rakhein. Gaari bohat bhaari ho, stamina khatam ho ya dhalaan ho to fail ho sakta hai. Isi se kinare par khari boat ya jet ski paani mein dhakel sakte hain.

02Kahin bhi baithein

Paidal Circle / B daba kar rakhein: ghaas ya ret par baith jayein ya deewar se tek lagayein. Halka sa dabaana crouch karta hai.

03Vehicle menu aur WAiNK

Gaari mein D-pad Left daba kar rakhein: engine, hazard lights, trunk, windows. D-pad Up daba kar rakhein WAiNK app ke liye, phir R1 / RB se gaari scan karein: class, lock aur tracker pata chalta hai.

04Police se chhupein

Gaari mein Circle / B daba kar rakhein to character neeche jhuk jata hai.

05Mazay ke controls

Bhaagte hue X / A se gaari ke hood par slide. R1 / RB se ishaara, right stick dabaa kar logon par chillana, L2 / LT se seeti. Settings mein Classic layout purane GTA wale controls deta hai. English guide: hidden controls.
