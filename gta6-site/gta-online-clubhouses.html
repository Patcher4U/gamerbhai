01Overview

Clubhouses are purchasable properties in Grand Theft Auto Online added as part of the Bikers update and expanded upon in The Criminal Enterprises update.

02Description

Clubhouses can be purchased from Maze Bank Foreclosures and can have multiple decorations, as well as optional extras.

Once the player selects the desired clubhouse for the first time, an introductory cutscene plays, where Malc (a character from The Lost and Damned) welcomes the player to the new clubhouse and explains how the Motorcycle Clubs and clubhouse features work.

There are recreational facilities available for the crew, including Darts, jukebox, Arm Wrestling, among others. Malc explains that the player needs some prospects to join to the club and how to establish a hierarchy. Then, Malc explains about jobs that appear on the notice board. The garage is a prominent feature of the club, where up to ten player-owned bikes can be stored. If the optional bike shop is purchased, it will be available in a corner of the garage. Malc explains that members of the MC still pay for the modifications to Zach Nelson, who runs the bike shop in the clubhouse. The Gun Locker (if purchased) works in the same way as its office counterpart, allowing players to store weapons and equip only what the player needs.

05Layout

There are two different layouts available depending on the site selected.

06Layout 1

A single-story building interior where the main entrance leads to the bar area, as well as the recreation room. Next to the bar area, there is the meeting room, where the MC convenes to start any of the MC jobs available. Opposite the entrance, there is an office room where the Open Road laptop and the owner's bed and wardrobe are located, as well as a bathroom accessed through a wooden door. Next to the recreation room, there is the garage area, divided into two sections. The first where up to ten of the owner's bikes can be stored and sorted via a vehicle management corona. The second section is where club members bikes are temporarily parked when they enter the clubhouse. The bike shop is located on the left side of the same garage door. After The Criminal Enterprises update, when the bike shop is owned, a customer bike can be parked at the rear of the member parking zone.

07Layout 2

A two-story interior layout where the main entrance leads to the garage area, with the bikes stored on the right side and the member parking and bike shop on the left. The meeting room is located next to the member parking area, between the bike shop and the bathroom, while the office room is located next to the player's bike storage area. The customer bike parking is right at the back. A set of stairs next to the office door lead to the second floor, where all the recreational features are located. The bar area is located in the corner just ahead of the stairs.

08Bar

The bar is staffed by the Clubhouse bartender Sindy, whom the player can interact with to order free shots of The Mount whiskey and, post-The Criminal Enterprises update, start a Bar Resupply mission. Tied to this mission is a cash bag located behind the office desk, where the player can collect their accumulated Bar Earnings, up to a maximum of $100,000.

09Jukebox

There is a jukebox in the bar area, which can play a selection of in-game radio stations, but with the ads and DJ intermissions removed. Changing the station costs $1 and can only be changed by the Clubhouse owner.

10List of stations available

  • "Classic Rock" - Los Santos Rock Radio
  • "Modern Rock" - Vinewood Boulevard Radio
  • "Hip Hop" - West Coast Classics
  • "Punk" - Channel X
  • "Media Player" - Media Player (added with Los Santos Tuners)

11Recreation

The dartboard, Arm-wrestling table, TV and lounge area are available to all visitors.

12Bike Workshop

The optional Bike Workshop adds Zach Nelson and works in the same way as Los Santos Customs and Benny's Original Motor Works, only that is exclusive for bikes. Players can access the bike shop by getting on any owned bike stored in the clubhouse. The same services are offered to the clubhouse owner whether they have registered as a MC President or not, and to the MC club members who have parked their bikes in the member parking area.

In The Criminal Enterprises update, the Bike Service was added which allows players to participate in a new side-line activity.

13MC Jobs

The clubrooms include a noticeboard that keeps track of various statistics for the MC. A variety of jobs will be randomly posted 3 at a time for the player to chose one.
