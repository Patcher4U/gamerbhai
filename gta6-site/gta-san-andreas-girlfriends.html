San Andreas Girlfriends

🏜️ Where to meet all six, what they like, and what CJ gets at 50% and 100%.

01How dating works

CJ can date six girlfriends. Only Millie matters for the story: you need her keycard for the casino heist (Key to Her Heart). Denise and Millie are met in missions; Helena, Katie, Michelle and Barbara become available after The Green Sabre or as the map opens up.

A good date raises her relationship meter by 5%, a bad one lowers it by 5%. Take her to the kind of place she likes, drive carefully, and bring her home on time. Gifts (like flowers) and a successful kiss add 1%. If you stop calling, the meter slowly drops and she may dump CJ.
