GTA 5 Spaceship Parts

🛸 All 50 locations, one line each. Spaceship Parts are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto V.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 50 devices scattered around San Andreas, which are claimed to have been dropped by a spaceship after it crashed.

Omega, a hippie from the Grand Senora Desert who claims to have been abducted by said spaceship in the day it crashed, can be encountered in Grand Senora Desert by Franklin Clinton after the mission Fame or Shame. He explains that aliens named him "Omega" and they were about to abduct him when their spaceship crashed. He asks Franklin to collect all 50 Spaceship Parts that are scattered throughout San Andreas, after he shows a spaceship part on his phone.

These collectibles are tied to Omega's Strangers and Freaks mission strand, unlocked after the mission Fame or Shame. Completing Omega's mission strand after collecting all 50 Spaceship Parts is required for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Spaceship Parts are made available upon starting the Strangers and Freaks mission Far Out.

03Rewards

Collecting all Spaceship Parts is required to unlock Omega's final Strangers and Freaks mission The Final Frontier, which will reward the player with the special Space Docker vehicle and the achievement "From Beyond the Stars". There is no additional reward for collecting each Spaceship Part.
