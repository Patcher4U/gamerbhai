GTA 6 on PS5 vs PS5 Pro vs Xbox Series X|S: Which Runs Best?

Every version of GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second, but they don't all hold it equally well. Here's the short version of Digital Foundry's testing.

01🥇 PS5 Pro: best

Holds around 30 FPS almost all the time (brief dips to about 24 FPS in big explosions), at about 1440p upscaled with PSSR. The sharpest and steadiest version.

02🥈 PS5 and Xbox Series X: tied

Around 1440p, with frame rates between 30 and the high 20s in combat and fast driving, and occasional drops into the low 20s. Series X has a tiny smoothness edge thanks to 120Hz VRR.

03🥉 Xbox Series S: weakest

Renders at 720p upscaled to about 1080p, with drops to around 20 FPS in heavy scenes and lower-quality lighting, shadows and textures. Still playable, but clearly behind.

04Any 60 FPS mode?

No. There's no performance mode on any console at launch, and the first patch didn't change that (see our patch notes). PC is still unannounced (PC status).
