01Shop Robberies – location 1

WhereEastbourne Way, Rockford Hills
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player enters the Bob Mulét barber shop and discovers that the place is being robbed. Wait until the robbers leave the premises and chase after them. They will try to escape in a car. Shoot them through the window or destroy the car to kill them. Retrieve the bag of money and return it to the shop, or keep it.
RewardKeep cash: $2,000. Return cash: No reward.

02Shop Robberies – location 2

WhereProsperity Street Promenade, Del Perro
Who can get itAll characters
What happensThe player enters the SubUrban clothing store and discovers that the place is being robbed. Wait until the robbers get out of the shop and try to escape on foot. Go after the robber with the bag of money and shoot him to recover it. Grab the bag of money and return it to the shop, or keep it.
RewardKeep cash: $2,000. Return cash: 25% off the next purchase at SubUrban.
