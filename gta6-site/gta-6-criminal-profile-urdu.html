GTA 6 Criminal Profile Kya Hai? Professional se Irredeemable Tak (Urdu/Hindi)

GTA 6 chupke se dekhta hai ke Jason aur Lucia crime kaise karte hain. Is ko Criminal Profile kehte hain. Ye Focus bonus deta hai aur zyada had se guzre to hamesha ke liye lock ho sakta hai.

015 levels

Professional (khamosh, bina maare), Hardened (pakde jaane par taaqat), Violent (police aur aam logon par hamla), Psycho (bila wajah tashaddud) aur Irredeemable (permanent lock). Kuch sites doosre level ko 'Aggressive' kehti hain.

02Psycho ki taraf kya le jata hai

Police ko maarna, aam logon ko nuqsaan pohanchana (khaas kar bhaagte ya bebas logon ko), paaltu ya kamzor janwaron ko maarna aur bila wajah tabahi. Dhamki dena (jaise store clerk) aur self-defence shumar nahi hote.

03Professional kaise rahein

Zaroorat na ho to violence na karein, nazar mein na aayein, non-lethal takedown istemal karein. Achhi khabar: Hardened, Violent aur Psycho se wapas aaya ja sakta hai.

04Toota hua icon = aakhri warning

Agar Psycho icon mein crack aa jaye to ruk jayein. Iske baad Irredeemable hai, jo normal game mein wapas nahi aata aur dono characters par asar karta hai. Rampage se pehle manual save zaroor karein.

05Iska faida

Har style alag tareeqe se Focus deta hai: khamosh players ko non-lethal takedowns par, violent players ko headshots par. English mein poori detail: Criminal Profile explained.
