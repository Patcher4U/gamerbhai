GTA 5 Letter Scraps

✉️ All 50 locations, one line each. Letter Scraps are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto V.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

In 1975, famous Vinewood starlet Leonora Johnson was brutally murdered, and her dismembered body was found at the Land Act Dam. Shortly after, Leonora's killer, sent a letter to Richards Majestic Productions founder David Richards, confessing his crime. The letter remained a secret until 2013.

One year prior, in 2012, David Richards destroyed the old confession letter weeks before his death. Richards' grandson Ira Richards revealed the existence of the confession letter to a Weazel News reporter one year after his grandfather's death, resulting in the search for the letter scraps.

The Letter Scraps are found strewn throughout San Andreas, appearing as white glowing pieces of paper laying on the floor. When the player is nearby, the sound of a piece of paper flapping in the wind can be heard, allowing them to locate the collectible. The game will automatically be saved after picking one up (unless the player has the "Autosave" option disabled).

02Availability

Letter Scraps are available upon completing the mission Repossession. They can be collected by all three protagonists. Chop can also help Franklin find the scraps at the nearest location.

The locations of all 50 Letter Scraps, including those the player has not found, is available to players with a Rockstar Games Social Club account; it can be viewed under the "Checklist" tab.

03Rewards

Collecting all 50 Letter Scraps reveals the truth behind the murder of actress Leonora Johnson, and will unlock the mission A Starlet in Vinewood for Franklin only, despite the fact Michael and Trevor can also collect them.
