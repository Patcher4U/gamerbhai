GTA Vice City Unique Stunt Jumps

🏍️ All 36 locations, one line each. Stunt Jumps are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 36 jumps located around Vice City. Each completed stunt jump earns the player a reward of $100 multiplied by the number of jumps completed (for the n-th jump, $100*n). The 36th jump earns the player $10,000.

Additional Insane Stunt Bonus payments can also be earned as the player completes each unique jump.

Some jumps are over water and pose extra danger since Tommy Vercetti cannot swim and will be wasted if the landing is missed.

Completing all 36 jumps is required for 100% completion.
