GTA 3 Hidden Packages

📦 All 100 locations, one line each. Hidden Packages are a collectible in Grand Theft Auto III.

✅ Tick each one as you find it – ticks are saved in this browser only.

01Description

There are 100 Hidden Packages located all around Liberty City, which the player must find and collect while on foot. These items cannot be collected while on a vehicle. They take the appearance of a white bale sealed with brown adhesive tape.

Collecting all Hidden Packages is a requirement for the 100% completion.

02Availability

The Hidden Packages are available upon gaining control of Claude during Give Me Liberty.

03Rewards

Each Hidden Package will award the player with $1,000. Collecting all packages alone will result in a total of $100,000 earned. An additional $1,000,000 reward is obtained upon collecting the last package, totaling a $1,100,000 reward.

For each 10 Hidden Packages collected, a permanent spawn point for a specific item is created in front of all safehouses. The player will be notified of each reward via pager.
