01Countryside Gang Fight

WhereMarina Drive, Alamo Sea
Who can get itMichael or Franklin only
What happensA man will be confronted at gunpoint (later shot at if the player is too late to react) by a couple of members of The Lost MC. After saving him, follow the man to his car. A new group of Lost members will appear on motorcycles and start shooting. Kill all of them and wait until the man leaves in his car to complete the random event.
Reward$1,000 for saving the victim.
