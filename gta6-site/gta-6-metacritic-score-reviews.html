GTA 6 Reviews: Metacritic 99 and What Critics Loved (and Didn't)

Grand Theft Auto VI didn't just review well. It landed on the very top of Metacritic's all-time list.

01The score

On PlayStation 5, GTA 6 holds a 99 Metascore from roughly 144–150 critic reviews. That ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest Metascore of all time. The Xbox Series X|S version also shows 99, from fewer reviews. On OpenCritic the Top Critic Average sits at 98–99, with nearly every critic recommending it.

02Who gave it what

IGN and GameSpot both gave it a perfect 10/10, and Eurogamer and GamesRadar+ reportedly gave 5/5. The lowest major score reported is a 9/10, so there isn't a single negative review among the big outlets.

03What critics loved

The size and detail of Leonida, the Jason and Lucia relationship at the heart of the story, and how alive Vice City feels came up again and again.

04The complaints

Some reviewers noted that a few long-running Rockstar habits remain, like tightly scripted missions. Performance is capped at 30 FPS on every console (see our performance comparison).

05Should you buy it?

If the scores matter to you, few games in history have scored higher. Check the price in your currency and our beginner guides before you start.
