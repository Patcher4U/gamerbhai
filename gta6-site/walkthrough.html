?Quick answers

How many missions are in GTA 6?

Guides count about 119 main story missions across a prologue and five chapters. Exact totals differ between guides because some count optional or repeated missions.

How many chapters does GTA 6 have?

A prologue plus five chapters: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, a Vice City chapter, Vice City Baby, and Top of the World, Ma.

Are there missable missions in GTA 6?

Yes. Some side missions in Chapters 1, 3 and 4 disappear if you move on in the story. See the side missions section.

Mission names and order are matched across several player guides and may be updated as more players finish the game. Unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
