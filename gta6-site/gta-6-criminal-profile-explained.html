GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: Professional to Irredeemable

GTA 6 quietly watches how Jason and Lucia commit crimes, not just whether they do. The Criminal Profile grades your style, gives Focus bonuses to match it, and can lock you into a permanent state if you go too far. Here's how it works.

01The five states

Guides based on the released game list five states: Professional (clean, quiet, non-lethal), Hardened (uses force when spotted), Violent (attacks cops and civilians), Psycho (extreme, pointless violence) and Irredeemable (a permanent lock). Some sites call the second state "Aggressive", so you may see both names.

02What pushes you toward Psycho

Killing cops, hurting civilians (especially ones running away or defenceless), killing pets or vulnerable animals and causing unnecessary destruction all move the profile up. Threats, like holding up a store clerk, and self-defence don't count against you, and a clean robbery isn't punished by itself.

03How to stay Professional

Avoid violence unless you're threatened, stay undetected, use non-lethal takedowns and only do what the job needs. The good news: Hardened, Violent and even Psycho can be walked back by playing cleaner for a while.

04The cracked icon: your last warning

If your Psycho icon starts to crack, stop. It's the warning before Irredeemable. Sources disagree on how many warning stages there are, so treat any crack as the final one.

05Irredeemable is permanent

Once the profile hits Irredeemable it doesn't come back in normal play, and it affects both characters. One guide says the only fix is loading a save from before it happened, so keep a manual save before you go on a rampage.

06What it changes

Each style earns Focus differently: quiet players get it for non-lethal takedowns, violent players for headshots. Psycho players reportedly can't surrender to police. GTABase says the profile plays a part in which ending you get, but exact story effects aren't fully confirmed yet. Jason and Lucia have separate profiles, but their actions affect each other when they're together.
