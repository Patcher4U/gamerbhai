01How random events work

Random events are short encounters that pop up while you explore Los Santos and Blaine County: a robbery, a car crash, a hitchhiker. A light blue dot on the minimap means someone needs help; a red dot means trouble. Help (or rob) them for cash, stat boosts and sometimes a new contact. 14 random events are needed for 100% completion. Many only appear once, and some are limited to one character or need the story to reach a certain point.
