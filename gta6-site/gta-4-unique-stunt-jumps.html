01Description

The player must complete all 50 jumps in order to achieve 100% completion in Grand Theft Auto IV.

On screen messages will inform the player of their progress towards completion of all of the jumps:

  • If the stunt jump is successful, the game will report "Stunt jump completed" then "XX stunt jumps left to do." Successful, already completed: "Stunt jump completed again"
  • Successful, already completed: "Stunt jump completed again"
  • If the stunt jump is unsuccessful, the game will report "Not good enough." Unsuccessful, already completed: "Not Good enough, but you have already completed this stunt jump"
  • Unsuccessful, already completed: "Not Good enough, but you have already completed this stunt jump"

Some jumps do not require Niko Bellic to make it a certain distance, instead they must simply land the jump. Other jumps have very strict requirements that are hard to determine. These details have been explained below where known. A jump can sometimes be marked successful even when Niko falls off on landing.

There are numerous locations with ramps around Liberty City that appear to be stunt jump sites, but actually aren't. Several missions provide opportunities to complete stunt jumps during chase sequences.

Jumps are greatly aided by use of a motorcycle. Some jumps are almost impossible without one. Two water jumps require use of a boat.

Once all 50 are completed, the player will receive the Dare Devil achievement and contributes 2.5% towards 100% completion.
