How Big Is the GTA 6 Map? Leonida vs GTA 5 Explained

Leonida is the biggest map Rockstar has ever built. But you'll see wildly different square-mile numbers online, so here's what's actually known.

01What Rockstar said

Rockstar's only official comparison is a ratio: Leonida is roughly twice the size of GTA 5's map, about three times Red Dead Redemption 2's, and nearly as large as the maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 combined.

02Why the square miles differ

There's no official square-mile figure. Estimates range from about 32 to 59 square miles depending on what size people assume GTA 5 was, and on whether water is counted. One outlier claims 120+, but it starts from a much bigger GTA 5 figure.

03The 6 regions

Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. See them all on our characters & map page.
