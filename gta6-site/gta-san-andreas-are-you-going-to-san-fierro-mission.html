02What happens

After being rewarded a garage in San Fierro, CJ meets The Truth at his farm and pays him for the weed. Later, both are ambushed by the police. Truth tells CJ that they must burn the weed and leave the place. Burn down The Truth's cannabis plantation and drive to San Fierro.
