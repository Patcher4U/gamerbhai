02What happens

CJ gained the trust of Millie Perkins and the keycard is yet to be received anytime. The next step is to establish a means of temporarily cutting the power supply to Las Venturas, so that the power could be disabled during the heist. CJ heads to the Sherman Dam to plant explosives on the generators. Plant explosive charges on the generators at the Sherman Dam.
