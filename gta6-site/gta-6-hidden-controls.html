GTA 6 Hidden Controls: Tricks the Game Never Explains

GTA 6 has a huge control scheme and the tutorial skips a lot of it. These are the most useful hidden controls, with PS5 and Xbox buttons (default layout).

01Flip a car back on its wheels

Car upside down? Walk up to its side and hold Square / X to push it over. It can fail if the car is too heavy, you run out of stamina or you're on a steep slope. The same move shoves a boat or jet ski off the sand into the water.

02Sit or rest anywhere

Hold Circle / B on foot to sit on grass or sand, lean against a wall or rest. A quick tap of the same button crouches instead.

03The vehicle controls menu

Hold D-pad Left in a car to open the vehicle menu: engine off/on, hazard lights, trunk and windows. It's new to the series.

04Scan cars with WAiNK

Hold D-pad Up to open the WAiNK app, then hold R1 / RB to scan a vehicle and see its class, whether it's locked and whether it's tracked. Handy before you steal something hot.

05Duck from the cops

In a car, hold Circle / B to duck down and stay out of sight.

06Slide over the hood

Run at a car and press X / A to slide over the hood. Press X / A right after climbing to chain jumps.

07Throw things

R2 / RT throws the object you're holding, yes, including at people.

08Talk back to Leonida

R1 / RB flips someone off, clicking the right stick yells at pedestrians and drivers, and holding L2 / LT whistles to get attention. Bike riders can bunnyhop with Circle / B.

09Classic layout

Miss the old GTA controls? There are three control schemes in the settings. Classic swaps Run and Crouch back to how older games played.
