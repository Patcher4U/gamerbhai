02GTA 6 in the United Kingdom: quick answers

How much is GTA 6 in the United Kingdom?

GTA 6 Standard Edition is $79.99 USD, which is about GBP 61.08 at the exchange rate on 22 November 2026. The Ultimate Edition ($99.99) is about GBP 76.35. Prices in your local store can be different because of regional pricing and taxes.

Is GTA 6 available on PC in the United Kingdom?

Not yet. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. Past Rockstar games came to PC 7 to 19 months after consoles.

Can I download GTA 6 for free in the United Kingdom?

No. Any website, app or video that offers a free GTA 6 download, APK or PC version is a scam and can steal your account or install malware. Buy only from official stores.

Where can I buy GTA 6 safely in the United Kingdom?

Buy from the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store (Xbox), Rockstar's own store, or a well-known retail shop. Be careful with cheap 'account sharing' or key offers on social media.
