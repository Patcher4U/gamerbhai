GTA 6 Sales: $3.79 Billion on Day One, the Biggest Launch Ever

GTA 6 is now the biggest entertainment launch in history, bigger than any movie or album.

01The official number

Take-Two (Rockstar's parent company) estimates about $3.79 billion in global retail sales through the end of launch day, 19 November 2026, with 32.5 million copies sold before launch. These are the company's own estimates, not audited revenue.

02The outside estimate

Analytics firm Sensor Tower put day one lower, at around 24.6 million digital copies and about $2.36 billion, including pre-orders. The two numbers measure different things, so both can be right.

03How it compares

For comparison, GTA 5 made about $800 million in its first day back in 2013. GTA 6 roughly quadrupled that and beat every movie opening weekend ever recorded.

04What it means for players

Huge sales mean Rockstar will keep supporting the game for years. The open questions are when GTA 6 Online and the PC version arrive.

Sources: Deadline · Notebookcheck. Facts checked 24 November 2026; details can change with updates. GamerBhai is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
