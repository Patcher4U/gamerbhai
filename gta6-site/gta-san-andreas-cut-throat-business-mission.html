02What happens

Madd Dogg finds out that the rival rapper OG Loc is at a video shoot and that the rhymes by Loc are actually from his book that CJ stole for Loc before. CJ and Madd Dogg decide to go to the shoot to interrupt Loc. Chase OG Loc to reclaim Madd Dogg's rhyme book.
